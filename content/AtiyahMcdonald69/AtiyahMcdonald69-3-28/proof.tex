\textbf{(i)} The set $D(g)$ is the image of $\operatorname{Spec}(A_g)$, and its complement $V(g)$ is the image of $\operatorname{Spec}(A/(g))$. Both are constructibly closed.

\textbf{(ii)} For distinct primes, choose $g$ belonging to one but not the other. The complementary clopen sets $D(g)$ and $V(g)$ separate them.

\textbf{(iii)} By (i), $C'\subseteq C$, so the identity is continuous. Its source is quasi-compact by \exref{3.27}, and its target is Hausdorff by (ii). Every closed subset of the source is quasi-compact, hence has closed image in the target. Thus the identity is a closed bijection and a homeomorphism. The asserted description of closed sets follows from the definition of $C$.

\textbf{(iv)} The space is quasi-compact and Hausdorff, hence compact. The clopen separations in (ii) show that a connected subset cannot contain two distinct points.
