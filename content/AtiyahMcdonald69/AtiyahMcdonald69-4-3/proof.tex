Let $\mathfrak{q}$ be $\mathfrak{p}$-primary and choose a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}\supseteq\mathfrak{p}$. By \exref{3.10}, $A_{\mathfrak{m}}$ is a field. By Proposition 4.8, $\mathfrak{q}A_{\mathfrak{m}}$ is proper and contracts to $\mathfrak{q}$. Hence it is zero. But $0=\mathfrak{m}A_{\mathfrak{m}}$ contracts to $\mathfrak{m}$, so $\mathfrak{q}=\mathfrak{m}$.
