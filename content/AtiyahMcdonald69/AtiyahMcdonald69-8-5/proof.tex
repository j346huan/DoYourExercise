Write $B=P(X)$ and $A=k[y_1,\ldots,y_r]$ as in \exref{5.16}. The algebra $B$ is integral and of finite type over $A$, hence finite. Choose $N$ module generators. Every fiber algebra $B\otimes_Ak(\mathfrak p)$ then has dimension at most $N$.

A finite-dimensional algebra over a field has at most its dimension many maximal ideals: for any finite collection of distinct maximal ideals, the Chinese remainder theorem gives a surjection onto the product of their nonzero residue fields. Thus each fiber has at most $N$ points by \exref{8.4}. Surjectivity follows from lying over.
