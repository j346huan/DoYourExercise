Localization commutes with finite sums and intersections of ideals. At every maximal ideal $\mathfrak p$, the ring $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is a discrete valuation ring, so its ideals are linearly ordered. Assume $\mathfrak b_{\mathfrak p}\subseteq\mathfrak c_{\mathfrak p}$. Then both sides of the first identity are $\mathfrak a_{\mathfrak p}\cap\mathfrak c_{\mathfrak p}$, and both sides of the second are $\mathfrak a_{\mathfrak p}+\mathfrak b_{\mathfrak p}$. The reverse inclusion is symmetric. Thus both identities hold after localization at every maximal ideal, and hence hold in $A$.
