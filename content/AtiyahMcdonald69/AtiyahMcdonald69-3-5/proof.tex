Localization commutes with taking the nilradical, so $\sqrt{(0)}_{\mathfrak p}=\sqrt{(0)_{A_{\mathfrak p}}}=0$ for every $\mathfrak p$. The local criterion for vanishing gives $\sqrt{(0)}=0$.

The corresponding assertion for integral domains is false. The ring $\mathbb F_2\times\mathbb F_2$ has two prime ideals, and localization at either gives $\mathbb F_2$, but $(1,0)(0,1)=0$.
