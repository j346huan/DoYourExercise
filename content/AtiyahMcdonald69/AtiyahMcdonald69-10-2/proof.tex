By \exref{10.1}, $A_n=\bigoplus_{j>n}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, so $\varprojlim A_n=\bigcap_nA_n=0$. The constant inverse system $A$ has limit $A$ and vanishing first derived limit, since its transition maps are surjective. Also $\varprojlim A/A_n=\prod_{j\ge1}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$.

The inverse-limit exact sequence therefore gives
\[0\to\bigoplus_{j\ge1}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\to\prod_{j\ge1}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\to\varprojlim{}^1A_n\to0.\]
The map from the direct sum to the product is the usual inclusion of the direct sum into the product, which is not surjective. Thus inverse limit is not right exact, and
\[\varprojlim{}^1A_n\cong\left(\prod_{j\ge1}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\right)\Big/\left(\bigoplus_{j\ge1}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\right).\]
