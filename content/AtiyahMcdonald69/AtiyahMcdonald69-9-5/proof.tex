If $M$ is flat, tensoring the injection $A\xrightarrow{a}A$ with $M$ shows that multiplication by every $0\ne a\in A$ is injective on $M$. Thus $M$ is torsion-free.

Conversely, if $M$ is torsion-free, then $M_{\mathfrak m}$ is finitely generated and torsion-free over $A_{\mathfrak m}$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, by \exref{3.13}. Since $A_{\mathfrak m}$ is a discrete valuation ring, the structure theorem over a principal ideal domain makes $M_{\mathfrak m}$ free. Now \exref{7.16} shows that $M$ is flat.
