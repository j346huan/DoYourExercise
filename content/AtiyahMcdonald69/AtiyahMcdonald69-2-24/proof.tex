(i)$\Rightarrow$(ii): Tensor a free resolution of $N$ with $M$. Flatness preserves exactness, so its homology vanishes in every positive degree.

(ii)$\Rightarrow$(iii) is immediate.

(iii)$\Rightarrow$(i): For an exact sequence $0\to N'\to N\to N''\to0$, the Tor exact sequence gives
\[0=\operatorname{Tor}_1^A(M,N'')\to M\otimes_A N'\to M\otimes_A N\to M\otimes_A N''\to0.\]
Thus tensoring with $M$ preserves short exact sequences, so $M$ is flat.
