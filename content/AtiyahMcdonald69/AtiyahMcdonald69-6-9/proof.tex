By \exref{6.8}, $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ is Noetherian, so it has finitely many irreducible components by \exref{6.7}. An irreducible closed subset has the form $V(\mathfrak p)$ for a prime $\mathfrak p$, and inclusion of such subsets reverses inclusion of the primes. Thus the irreducible components correspond exactly to minimal primes, proving finiteness.
