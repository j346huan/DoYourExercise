Finite subsets are directed by inclusion, and inserting factors of $1$ gives compatible ring homomorphisms. Thus \exref{2.21} gives a ring structure on $B$. The structure map $B_\varnothing=A\to B$ makes it an $A$-algebra. For every finite $J$, the composite $A=B_\varnothing\to B_J\to B$ is this same map by compatibility. Hence each $B_J\to B$ is an $A$-algebra homomorphism.
