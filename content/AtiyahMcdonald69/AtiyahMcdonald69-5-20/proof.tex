Let $K=\operatorname{Frac}A$. By \exref{5.16}, $(A\setminus\{0\})^{-1}B$ is integral over a polynomial subalgebra $K[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$. Multiplying the $x_i$ by a common nonzero denominator in $A$ gives elements $y_i\in B$, still algebraically independent over $K$, with $K[y_1,\ldots,y_n]=K[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$.

Choose finitely many $A$-algebra generators of $B$. Each satisfies a monic equation over $K[y_1,\ldots,y_n]$. Let $s\neq0$ be the product of the denominators in $A$ of all coefficients occurring in these finitely many equations. Their coefficients then lie in $A_s[y_1,\ldots,y_n]$. Since $B_s$ is generated over this ring by the same generators, it is integral over it.
