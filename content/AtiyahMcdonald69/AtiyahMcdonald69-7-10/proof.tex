Choose generators $m_1,\ldots,m_r$ of $M$. The map $A/\operatorname{Ann}(M)\to M^r$ given by $a\mapsto(am_1,\ldots,am_r)$ is injective, so $A/\operatorname{Ann}(M)$ is Noetherian. By Hilbert's basis theorem, its polynomial ring is Noetherian. The constant polynomials $m_1,\ldots,m_r$ generate $M[x]$ over this ring. Hence $M[x]$ is Noetherian, and the same holds for its $A[x]$-module structure.
