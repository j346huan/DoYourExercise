If $\mathfrak{m}$ is maximal in $k[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$, then $k[t_1,\ldots,t_n]/\mathfrak{m}$ is a field finitely generated as a $k$-algebra. By \exref{5.18}, it is finite algebraic over $k$, hence equals $k$ since $k$ is algebraically closed. Writing $a_i$ for the image of $t_i$, we obtain $\mathfrak{m}=(t_1-a_1,\ldots,t_n-a_n)$. Every proper ideal lies in a maximal ideal, so it has a common zero.
