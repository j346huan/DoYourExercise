The ring $B$ is again a valuation ring, hence local. Let $\mathfrak{p}$ be the contraction of its maximal ideal. Every element of $A\setminus\mathfrak{p}$ is a unit in $B$, so $A_{\mathfrak{p}}\subseteq B$.

If $b\in B\setminus A$, then $b^{-1}\in A$ and is a unit in $B$, so $b^{-1}\notin\mathfrak{p}$. Thus $b=1/b^{-1}\in A_{\mathfrak{p}}$. Elements of $A$ already belong to $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$, giving $B=A_{\mathfrak{p}}$.
