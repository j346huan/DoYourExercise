The completion $\widehat A$ is flat over $A$ by Proposition 10.14. It is faithfully flat exactly when $\widehat A/\mathfrak{m}\widehat A\ne0$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$.

By Proposition 10.13, $\widehat A/\mathfrak{m}\widehat A$ is the $\mathfrak{a}$-adic completion of $A/\mathfrak{m}$. If $\mathfrak{a}\subseteq\mathfrak{m}$, this completion is $A/\mathfrak{m}$; otherwise it is zero. Thus faithful flatness is equivalent to $\mathfrak{a}$ being contained in every maximal ideal.
