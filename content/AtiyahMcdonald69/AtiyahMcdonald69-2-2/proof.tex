Tensoring $0\to\mathfrak{a}\to A\to A/\mathfrak{a}\to0$ with $M$ gives an exact sequence $\mathfrak{a}\otimes_A M\to M\to(A/\mathfrak{a})\otimes_A M\to0$. The first map sends $a\otimes m$ to $am$, so its image is $\mathfrak{a}M$. Hence the required isomorphism sends $(a+\mathfrak{a})\otimes m$ to $am+\mathfrak{a}M$.
