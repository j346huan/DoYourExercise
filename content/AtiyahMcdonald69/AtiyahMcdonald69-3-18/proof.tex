The ring $B_{\mathfrak p}$ is flat over $A_{\mathfrak p}$ by base change. Since $B_{\mathfrak q}$ is a localization of $B_{\mathfrak p}$, it is flat over $B_{\mathfrak p}$ and hence over $A_{\mathfrak p}$. Moreover, $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ extends into the proper ideal $\mathfrak qB_{\mathfrak q}$. Since $A_{\mathfrak p}$ has only one maximal ideal, condition (iii) of \exref{3.16} holds. Thus $B_{\mathfrak q}$ is faithfully flat over $A_{\mathfrak p}$, and the map on spectra is surjective.
