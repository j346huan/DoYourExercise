Every primary ideal between $\mathfrak q$ and $\mathfrak p$ has radical $\mathfrak p$. Localization and contraction identify these ideals with the ideals between $\mathfrak qA_{\mathfrak p}$ and $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$.

The quotient $A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak qA_{\mathfrak p}$ is Noetherian local of dimension zero, hence Artinian. Every proper ideal in it is primary. The chains in question therefore correspond exactly to chains of submodules from zero to its maximal ideal. This module has finite length, and its maximal chains are composition series. The Jordan–Hölder theorem gives the required bound and equality of lengths.
