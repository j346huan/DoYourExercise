Let $R=\mathbb{Q}[[t]]$, $E=\mathbb{Q}((t))/\mathbb{Q}[[t]]$, and give $A=R\oplus E$ the multiplication $(r,e)(s,f)=(rs,rf+se)$. An element $(r,e)$ is a unit exactly when $r$ is a unit of $R$: in that case its inverse is $(r^{-1},-r^{-2}e)$. Thus $A$ is local with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}=tR\oplus E$.

Since $tE=E$, we have $\mathfrak{m}=(t,0)A$ and $\mathfrak{m}^n=t^nR\oplus E$. Hence $A/\mathfrak{m}^n\cong R/t^nR$, so $\widehat A\cong R$ is Noetherian. The completion map is the projection $A\to R$, making $\widehat A$ a cyclic $A$-module.

The nonzero ideal $I=0\oplus E$ satisfies $\mathfrak{m}I=I$. If $A$ were Noetherian, $I$ would be finitely generated, and Nakayama's lemma would give $I=0$, a contradiction.
