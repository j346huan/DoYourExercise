Let $Y\subseteq X$, and let $U_1\subseteq U_2\subseteq\cdots$ be open in $Y$. Write $U_n=Y\cap V_n$ with $V_n$ open in $X$. Replacing $V_n$ by $V_1\cup\cdots\cup V_n$ gives an ascending chain with the same intersections with $Y$. It stabilizes, so the chain $U_n$ stabilizes.

Given an open cover of $X$, the finite unions of its members have a maximal element. If this union were not $X$, another member of the cover would enlarge it. Thus finitely many members cover $X$.
