For each $m$, complete $0\to\mathfrak{b}^mM\to M\to M/\mathfrak{b}^mM\to0$ in the $\mathfrak{a}$-adic topology. Exactness and Proposition 10.13 identify the image of the completed first term with $\mathfrak{b}^mM^{\mathfrak{a}}$, giving
\[M^{\mathfrak{a}}/\mathfrak{b}^mM^{\mathfrak{a}}\cong\varprojlim_n M/(\mathfrak{a}^nM+\mathfrak{b}^mM).\]
Taking the inverse limit over $m$ and using the cofinal diagonal in $\mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N}$ yields
\[(M^{\mathfrak{a}})^{\mathfrak{b}}\cong\varprojlim_n M/(\mathfrak{a}^nM+\mathfrak{b}^nM).\]
The inclusions $(\mathfrak{a}+\mathfrak{b})^{2n}\subseteq\mathfrak{a}^n+\mathfrak{b}^n\subseteq(\mathfrak{a}+\mathfrak{b})^n$ show that this filtration defines the $(\mathfrak{a}+\mathfrak{b})$-adic topology. Thus the last limit is $M^{\mathfrak{a}+\mathfrak{b}}$.
