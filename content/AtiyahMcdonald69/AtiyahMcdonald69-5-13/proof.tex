Lying-over and \exref{5.12} show that this set is nonempty. Let $\mathfrak{p}_1,\mathfrak{p}_2$ be two of its elements. For $x\in\mathfrak{p}_1$, the invariant product $\prod_{\sigma\in G}\sigma(x)$ belongs to $\mathfrak{p}$, hence to $\mathfrak{p}_2$. Primality gives $\sigma(x)\in\mathfrak{p}_2$ for some $\sigma$, so $\mathfrak{p}_1\subseteq\bigcup_{\sigma\in G}\sigma(\mathfrak{p}_2)$. By finite prime avoidance, $\mathfrak{p}_1\subseteq\sigma(\mathfrak{p}_2)$ for some $\sigma$. Both primes contract to $\mathfrak{p}$, so incomparability for integral extensions gives equality. Thus the action is transitive, and the set has at most $|G|$ elements.
