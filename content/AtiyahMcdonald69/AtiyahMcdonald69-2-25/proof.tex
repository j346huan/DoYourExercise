For every $A$-module $M$, flatness of $N''$ and the Tor exact sequence give
\[0=\operatorname{Tor}_2^A(M,N'')\to\operatorname{Tor}_1^A(M,N')\to\operatorname{Tor}_1^A(M,N)\to\operatorname{Tor}_1^A(M,N'')=0.\]
Hence the middle map is an isomorphism. By symmetry of Tor and \exref{2.24}, $N'$ is flat exactly when $N$ is flat.
