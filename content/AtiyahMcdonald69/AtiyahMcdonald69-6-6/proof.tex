\textbf{(ii)} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{(iii)}. Write an open cover of $Y\subseteq X$ as $Y\cap U_i$ with $U_i$ open in $X$. The open set $\bigcup_iU_i$ is quasi-compact and is covered by the $U_i$, so finitely many of them cover it and hence cover $Y$.

\textbf{(iii)} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{(i)}. An ascending chain of open subsets covers its union. Quasi-compactness gives a finite subcover, so one member already equals the union and the chain stabilizes.
