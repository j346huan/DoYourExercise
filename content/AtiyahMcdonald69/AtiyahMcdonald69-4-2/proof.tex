Let $\mathfrak{a}=\bigcap_{i=1}^n\mathfrak{q}_i$ be a minimal primary decomposition, with $\mathfrak{p}_i=r(\mathfrak{q}_i)$. Since $\mathfrak{a}$ is radical, $\mathfrak{a}=\bigcap_{i=1}^n\mathfrak{p}_i$. Removing the primes that contain another leaves a minimal primary decomposition with no embedded primes. The first uniqueness theorem gives the assertion.
