Fix $0\neq v\in B$. The algebra $B_v$ is finitely generated over $A$, so \exref{5.21} gives $0\neq s\in A$. Since $J(A)=0$, choose a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ not containing $s$. The map $A\to A/\mathfrak{m}$ extends to $g:B_v\to\Omega$, where $\Omega$ is an algebraic closure of $A/\mathfrak{m}$.

Because $v$ is invertible, $g(v)\neq0$. The image $g(B)$ contains $A/\mathfrak{m}$ and is a domain algebraic over this field, hence is a field. Thus $\ker(g|_B)$ is a maximal ideal of $B$ avoiding $v$. Every nonzero $v$ is avoided by a maximal ideal, so $J(B)=0$.
