The fiber over $\mathfrak p$ is homeomorphic to $\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_Ak(\mathfrak p))$ by \exref{3.21}. Its coordinate ring is a finitely generated algebra over $k(\mathfrak p)$, hence Noetherian. By \exref{8.2} and \exref{8.3}, this spectrum is discrete iff the algebra is finite-dimensional; in that case the spectrum is also finite.

If $B$ is finite over $A$, its generators span every fiber algebra over the corresponding residue field, proving the first implication.

The integral extension $\mathbb Q\to\overline{\mathbb Q}$ has a one-point spectral fiber, but it is not finite. Indeed, its subextensions contain roots of the Eisenstein polynomials $t^n-2$ for arbitrarily large $n$, so its degree over $\mathbb Q$ is infinite.
