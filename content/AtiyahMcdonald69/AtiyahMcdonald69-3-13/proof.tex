Suppose $(a/s)(m/t)=0$ with $a/s\ne0$. Then $uam=0$ for some $u\in S$. Since $ua\ne0$, we have $m\in T(M)$. Conversely, if $am=0$ for some $a\ne0$, then $a/1\ne0$ in $S^{-1}A$ and $(a/1)(m/t)=0$. This proves the equality.

In particular, $T(M)_{\mathfrak p}=T(M_{\mathfrak p})$. The local criterion for a module to vanish now gives both equivalences, since vanishing can be checked at maximal ideals.
