Every finite sum of elements of the $M_i$ lies in one $M_k$, so their union is a submodule and equals their sum. The inclusions induce $\alpha:\varinjlim_iM_i\to\bigcup_iM_i$ by \exref{2.16}. It is surjective. If $\alpha(\mu_i(x))=x=0$, then $\mu_i(x)=0$, so it is injective by \exref{2.15}.

The finitely generated submodules of any module form such a directed family, and their union is the whole module, since each element generates a finitely generated submodule.
