The ring $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ has only one prime ideal, so every nonunit is nilpotent. Hence $(0)$ is $\mathfrak{p}A_{\mathfrak{p}}$-primary, and its contraction $S_{\mathfrak{p}}(0)$ is $\mathfrak{p}$-primary. Every $\mathfrak{p}$-primary ideal is the contraction of its extension to $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$, so contains $S_{\mathfrak{p}}(0)$.

By \exref{4.10}, $S_{\mathfrak{p}}(0)\subseteq\mathfrak{p}$; thus $\mathfrak{a}$ lies in the intersection of the minimal primes, which is the nilradical.

Suppose $(0)=\bigcap_i\mathfrak{q}_i$ is a minimal primary decomposition. For each minimal associated prime $\mathfrak{p}_i$, localization at $\mathfrak{p}_i$ discards all other components, so $S_{\mathfrak{p}_i}(0)=\mathfrak{q}_i$. Hence $\mathfrak{a}$ is the intersection of the isolated components. This intersection is zero if all components are isolated; conversely, if it is zero, irredundancy leaves no embedded component.
