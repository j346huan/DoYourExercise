The open subsets $D(x)$ and $D(y)$ cover $X$, so inside $k(x,y)$,
\[\mathcal O(X)=k[x,y]_x\cap k[x,y]_y=k[x,y].\]
The last equality follows by unique factorization, since a reduced denominator dividing both a power of $x$ and a power of $y$ is a unit. If $X$ were affine, the inclusion $X\to\mathbb A^2$, whose pullback is this isomorphism, would be an isomorphism by (3.7). This contradicts its image omitting the origin.
