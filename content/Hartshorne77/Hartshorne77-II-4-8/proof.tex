(e) The graph $\Gamma_f:X\to X\times_ZY$ is a base change of the closed immersion $\Delta_g:Y\to Y\times_ZY$. The projection $X\times_ZY\to Y$ is a base change of $g\circ f$. Their composite is $f$, so (a)--(c) give the claim.

(f) The composite $X_{\mathrm{red}}\to X\to Y$ has the property by (a) and (b). It factors through $f_{\mathrm{red}}:X_{\mathrm{red}}\to Y_{\mathrm{red}}$, and $Y_{\mathrm{red}}\to Y$ is a closed immersion, hence separated. Apply (e).
