A finite morphism is of finite type. Over an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}A$ of the target, write its inverse image as $\operatorname{Spec}B$. The diagonal is induced by the surjection $B\otimes_AB\to B$, $b\otimes c\mapsto bc$, so the morphism is separated.

After any affine base change $A\to C$, the algebra $B\otimes_AC$ is finite over $C$. Thus finite morphisms are stable under arbitrary base change. A finite ring map is integral, and the image of $V(I)\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}B$ is $V(I\cap A)$ by lying over. Hence finite morphisms are universally closed, and therefore proper.
