Put $R=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$. Since $f$ is nonconstant and square-free, it is not a square in $\operatorname{Frac}R$. Thus $A$ is a domain with fraction field $K=\operatorname{Frac}(R)(z)$, where $z^2=f$.

Let $g+hz\in K$ be integral over $R$. Its conjugate $g-hz$ is integral, so $2g$ is integral over $R$. Since $2$ is invertible and $R$ is integrally closed, $g\in R$. Then $hz$ is integral, and therefore $h^2f\in R$. For every irreducible polynomial $p\in R$,
\[2v_p(h)+v_p(f)\geq0,\qquad v_p(f)\in\{0,1\}.\]
Hence $v_p(h)\geq0$ for every $p$, so $h\in R$. Conversely, every $g+hz$ with $g,h\in R$ is integral over $R$. Thus $A$ is the integral closure of $R$ in $K$, and is integrally closed.
