Assume $i=0,j=1$. Write a regular function as $F/G\in K(\mathbb P^n)$ with relatively prime homogeneous $F,G$ of the same degree. If an irreducible homogeneous factor $h$ divides $G$, then $Z(h)$ is not contained in $Z(F)$, and cannot be contained in $H_0\cap H_1$ by dimension. Choose $P\in Z(h)$ outside their union. At $P$, the function also has an expression $A/B$ with $B(P)\ne0$. The identity $FB=GA$ and coprimality imply $h\mid B$, a contradiction. Thus $G$ is constant, and homogeneity forces $F$ to be constant as well.
