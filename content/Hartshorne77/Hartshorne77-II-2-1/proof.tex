The map $\operatorname{Spec}A_f\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ identifies its points with the prime ideals of $A$ not containing $f$, and carries $D(a/f^n)$ onto $D(a)\cap D(f)$. It is therefore a homeomorphism onto $D(f)$. On these distinguished opens the induced maps on sections are the canonical isomorphisms $(A_f)_{a/f^n}\cong A_{af}$. They commute with restrictions, so identify the structure sheaf with $\mathcal O_X|_{D(f)}$.
