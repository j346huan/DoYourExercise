On a nonempty open subset, $f$ defines the morphism $P\mapsto(f(P):1)$ to $\mathbb{P}^1$. Proposition 6.8 extends it uniquely over the finitely many omitted points. If it omitted a point of $\mathbb{P}^1$, a projective linear change of coordinates would give a nonconstant morphism $Y\to\mathbb{A}^1$, contradicting $\mathcal O(Y)=k$. Hence it is surjective. Each fiber is a proper closed subset of the irreducible curve $Y$, so it is finite.
