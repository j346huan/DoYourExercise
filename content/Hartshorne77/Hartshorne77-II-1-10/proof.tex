Compatible morphisms $\mathscr F_i\to\mathscr G$ induce, for every open $U$, a unique homomorphism $\varinjlim_i\mathscr F_i(U)\to\mathscr G(U)$. These commute with restriction by uniqueness, so they define a morphism from the direct-limit presheaf to $\mathscr G$. The universal property of sheafification extends it uniquely to $\varinjlim_i\mathscr F_i\to\mathscr G$. Its composites with the canonical maps from the $\mathscr F_i$ are the given morphisms, and uniqueness follows from the two universal properties.
