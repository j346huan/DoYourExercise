(a) In coordinates $(x,z,w,y)=(a_0b_0,a_0b_1,a_1b_0,a_1b_1)$, the Segre image is $Z(xy-zw)$ by \exref{I.2.14}.

(b) For $t=(a_0:a_1)$, let $L_t=\psi(\{t\}\times\mathbb P^1)$ and $M_t=\psi(\mathbb P^1\times\{t\})$. These are lines because their coordinates depend linearly on the varying pair. Injectivity of $\psi$ shows that distinct lines in either family are disjoint, and $L_t\cap M_u=\{\psi(t,u)\}$.

(c) The diagonal has image $\{(s^2:t^2:st:st)\}$, a conic contained in $Q\cap Z(z-w)$, so this is a curve outside the two families. Its inverse image is the diagonal in $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$, which is not closed in the product topology: every nonempty product of cofinite open subsets meets it. Hence $\psi$ is not a homeomorphism for the product topology.
