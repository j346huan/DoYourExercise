(i)$\Rightarrow$(ii) Write $\operatorname{Spec}A=U\coprod V$ with $U,V$ nonempty and open. The sections $1$ on $U$ and $0$ on $V$ glue to $e_1\in A$; set $e_2=1-e_1$. Their restrictions show $e_1e_2=0$ and $e_i^2=e_i$. Neither is zero, since a nonempty scheme has nonzero unit section.

(ii)$\Rightarrow$(iii) The map $A\to e_1A\times e_2A$, $a\mapsto(e_1a,e_2a)$, is an isomorphism with inverse $(b,c)\mapsto b+c$. Each factor is a nonzero ring with identity $e_i$.

(iii)$\Rightarrow$(ii) Take $e_1=(1,0)$ and $e_2=(0,1)$.

(ii)$\Rightarrow$(i) The closed subsets $V(e_1)$ and $V(e_2)$ are disjoint and cover the spectrum. They are nonempty, since a nonzero idempotent cannot be nilpotent, so $D(e_i)=V(e_{3-i})$ is nonempty by \exref{II.2.18}(a).
