Choose an affine open neighborhood $U$ of $P$. Prime ideals of $\mathcal O_{P,X}=A(U)_{\mathfrak m_P}$ correspond to prime ideals $\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak m_P$ in $A(U)$, hence to closed subvarieties of $U$ containing $P$. Taking closure in $X$ and intersecting with $U$ are inverse operations on such subvarieties, giving the required correspondence.
