Let $d=\deg H$. Under the $d$-uple embedding, the defining polynomial of $H$ becomes a linear form, defining a hyperplane $L\subseteq\mathbb P^N$. Thus $\rho_d(\mathbb P^n\setminus H)=\rho_d(\mathbb P^n)\cap(\mathbb P^N\setminus L)$ is closed in $\mathbb P^N\setminus L\cong\mathbb A^N$. By \exref{I.3.4}, it is isomorphic to $\mathbb P^n\setminus H$, which is therefore affine.
