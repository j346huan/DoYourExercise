If $s_P=0$, there is an open neighborhood $V\subset U$ of $P$ on which $s$ vanishes. Then $s_Q=0$ for every $Q\in V$. Thus $\{P\in U:s_P=0\}$ is open and $\operatorname{Supp}s$ is closed in $U$.

The support of a sheaf need not be closed: for the inclusion $j:(0,1)\to\mathbb R$, the extension by zero $j_!\underline{\mathbb Z}$ has support $(0,1)$ by \exref{II.1.19}.
