Choose affine open covers $V=\bigcup_iV_i$ and $W=\bigcup_jW_j$. The products $V_i\times W_j$ form an affine open cover of $V\times W$, with coordinate rings $A(V_i)\otimes_kA(W_j)$ by \exref{I.3.15}. Hence
\[t(V_i\times W_j)\cong\operatorname{Spec}(A(V_i)\otimes_kA(W_j))\cong t(V_i)\times_{\operatorname{Spec}k}t(W_j).\]
These are the canonical isomorphisms determined by the two projections, so they agree after restricting to overlaps. They glue to $t(V\times W)\cong t(V)\times_{\operatorname{Spec}k}t(W)$.
