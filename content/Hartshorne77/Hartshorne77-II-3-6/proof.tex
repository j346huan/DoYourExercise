Every nonempty affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ contains the generic point $\xi$, because its complement is closed and proper. Since $X$ is integral, $A$ is a domain and $\xi$ corresponds to $(0)$. Therefore $\mathcal O_{X,\xi}=A_{(0)}=\operatorname{Frac}A$, a field independent of the chosen affine open.
