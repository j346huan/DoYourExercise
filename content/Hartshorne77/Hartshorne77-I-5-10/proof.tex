(a) The least number of generators of $\mathfrak m$ is $\dim_k\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$, by Nakayama's lemma. This is at least $\dim\mathcal O_{P,X}=\dim X$, with equality exactly when the local ring is regular. Dualizing does not change dimension.

(b) Pullback gives a local $k$-algebra homomorphism $\mathcal O_{\varphi(P),Y}\to\mathcal O_{P,X}$, hence a linear map $\mathfrak m_{\varphi(P)}/\mathfrak m_{\varphi(P)}^2\to\mathfrak m_P/\mathfrak m_P^2$. Its dual is $T_P(\varphi)$. These maps respect identities and compositions because pullbacks do.

(c) Under $\mathcal O_{0,X}\cong k[y]_{(y)}$, the pullback of $x$ is $y^2$. Therefore the induced map $(x)/(x^2)\to(y)/(y^2)$ is zero, and its dual $T_0(\varphi)$ is zero.
