The graph $\Gamma_f:X\to X\times_SY$ is a base change of the closed immersion $\Delta_Y:Y\to Y\times_SY$, hence is a closed immersion. Restricting to $Z$ factors $f|_Z$ as a closed immersion $Z\to Z\times_SY$ followed by the proper projection $Z\times_SY\to Y$. Thus $f|_Z$ is proper, and $f(Z)$ is closed.

Let $W$ be its image subscheme. Then $W$ is a closed subscheme of $Y$, so it is separated and of finite type over $S$. The map $Z\to W$ is surjective and remains so after every base change: a nonempty fiber over a field remains nonempty after a field extension. For any $T\to S$ and any closed subset $C\subseteq W_T$, its inverse image in $Z_T$ is closed and has the same image in $T$. That image is closed because $Z_T\to T$ is proper. Hence $W\to S$ is universally closed, and therefore proper.
