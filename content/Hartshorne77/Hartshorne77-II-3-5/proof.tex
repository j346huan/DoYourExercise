(a) For $y\in V=\operatorname{Spec}B$, write $f^{-1}(V)=\operatorname{Spec}A$ with $A$ finite over $B$. By \exref{II.3.10}(a), the fiber has the same points as $\operatorname{Spec}(A\otimes_Bk(y))$. This ring is finite-dimensional over $k(y)$, hence artinian and has finitely many prime ideals.

(b) In the affine situation, $A$ is integral over the image of $B$. For an ideal $I\subseteq A$, the injection $B/\varphi^{-1}(I)\to A/I$ is integral. Lying over gives $f(V(I))=V(\varphi^{-1}(I))$, so the affine morphism is closed. By \exref{II.3.4}, this argument applies over every affine open of $Y$, and closedness can be checked on such a cover.

(c) Consider $X=\operatorname{Spec}k[x,x^{-1}]\coprod\operatorname{Spec}k$ mapping to $\operatorname{Spec}k[x]$ by the open immersion on the first component and $x\mapsto0$ on the second. It is surjective and of finite type, and every fiber has one point. The first component is closed in $X$ but has nonclosed image $D(x)$, so (b) shows that the morphism is not finite.
