(a) Put $R=\mathcal O_{P,Y}$. This is a noetherian normal domain of dimension at least $2$, so $R=\bigcap_{\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak p=1}R_{\mathfrak p}$ inside $K(Y)$. A height-one prime corresponds to a codimension-one subvariety through $P$, which contains points other than $P$. At such a point, a local expression for $f$ has denominator not vanishing identically on that subvariety. Hence $f\in R_{\mathfrak p}$ for every height-one prime, so $f\in R$. Choose a representative near $P$ and glue it to $f$ on $Y\setminus\{P\}$; the representatives agree on their overlap because they define the same rational function.

(b) On $Y=\mathbb A^1$, the function $1/x$ is regular away from $0$ but does not belong to $k[x]_{(x)}$, so it does not extend.
