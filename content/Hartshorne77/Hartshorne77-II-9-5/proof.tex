Choose an ideal of definition $\mathcal J$. The images of the global generators generate the coherent sheaf $\mathcal F/\mathcal J\mathcal F$ on the noetherian scheme $(\mathfrak X,\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X}/\mathcal J)$. Around each point finitely many suffice, and quasi-compactness gives a finite set of global sections generating this quotient everywhere. Let $\mathcal G$ be the cokernel of the resulting map $\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X}^r\to\mathcal F$. It is coherent by (9.9), and $\mathcal G=\mathcal J\mathcal G$, hence $\mathcal G=\mathcal J^n\mathcal G$ for every $n$. By (9.6), $\mathcal G\cong\varprojlim_n\mathcal G/\mathcal J^n\mathcal G=0$.
