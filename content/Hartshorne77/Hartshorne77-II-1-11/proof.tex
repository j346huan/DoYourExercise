Every open subset of a noetherian space is quasi-compact. Suppose a section of the direct-limit presheaf on $U$, represented by $s\in\mathscr F_i(U)$, vanishes on an open cover. Choose a finite subcover and a common later index at which all the restrictions of $s$ vanish. The sheaf axiom at that index makes $s$ zero, proving uniqueness of gluing.

For existence, take compatible sections on a cover and choose a finite subcover. Their representatives can be moved to one common index. There are only finitely many pairwise overlaps, so move to a further common index at which all overlap equalities hold. The sheaf axiom there glues the representatives to a section on $U$. Its restrictions agree with the sections on the entire original cover by the uniqueness just proved. Thus the presheaf is already a sheaf, and in particular $\Gamma(X,\varinjlim_i\mathscr F_i)=\varinjlim_i\Gamma(X,\mathscr F_i)$.
