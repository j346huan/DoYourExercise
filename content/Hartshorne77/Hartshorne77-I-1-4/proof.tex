The projections are Zariski continuous, so the Zariski topology on $\mathbb A^2$ contains the product topology. The complement of $V(x-y)$ is Zariski open. Every nonempty product $U\times V$ of open subsets of $\mathbb A^1$ meets the diagonal, since $U$ and $V$ are cofinite and $U\cap V\ne\varnothing$. Hence this complement is not open in the product topology.
