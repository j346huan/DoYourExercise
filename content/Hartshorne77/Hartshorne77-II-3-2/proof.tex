Suppose $V_i=\operatorname{Spec}B_i$ is an affine open cover such that $f^{-1}(V_i)$ is quasi-compact. Choose a finite affine open cover $U_{ij}=\operatorname{Spec}A_{ij}$ of each $f^{-1}(V_i)$. For $b\in B_i$, its inverse image in $U_{ij}$ is a distinguished open, hence quasi-compact. Thus $f^{-1}(D_{V_i}(b))$ is a finite union of quasi-compact subsets and is quasi-compact.

Any affine open $V\subseteq Y$ has a cover by distinguished opens of the $V_i$ contained in $V$. Since $V$ is quasi-compact, finitely many suffice; their inverse images cover $f^{-1}(V)$ and are quasi-compact. The converse is immediate.
