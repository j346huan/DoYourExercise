The points are $(0)$, with residue field $\mathbb F_p(x)$, and $(f)$ for monic irreducible polynomials $f\in\mathbb F_p[x]$, with residue field $\mathbb F_{p^{\deg f}}$. The proper closed subsets are the finite sets of nonzero primes.

Let $N_n$ be the number of monic irreducible polynomials of degree $n$. The polynomial $x^{p^n}-x$ has distinct roots and is the product of the monic irreducible polynomials whose degrees divide $n$. Comparing degrees gives $p^n=\sum_{d\mid n}dN_d$, hence
\[N_n=\frac1n\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)p^{n/d}.\]
Thus there are $N_n$ points with residue field $\mathbb F_{p^n}$, and one with residue field $\mathbb F_p(x)$.
