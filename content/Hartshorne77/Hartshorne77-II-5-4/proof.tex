On an affine open set, write $\mathcal F\cong\widetilde M$. A presentation $A^{(I)}\to A^{(J)}\to M\to0$ gives $\mathcal O_X^{(I)}\to\mathcal O_X^{(J)}\to\mathcal F\to0$, since association of sheaves is exact and commutes with direct sums. Conversely, free sheaves are quasi-coherent, and cokernels of maps between quasi-coherent sheaves are quasi-coherent.

If $X$ is noetherian, a finite module over an affine coordinate ring is finitely presented. Thus a coherent sheaf has such a presentation with both free sheaves of finite rank. Conversely, the cokernel of a map between finite free sheaves is coherent.
