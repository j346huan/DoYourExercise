(a) For $f:\mathbb A_k^1\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, the sheaf $\mathcal O_{\mathbb A_k^1}$ is coherent, while $f_*\mathcal O_{\mathbb A_k^1}$ corresponds to the infinite-dimensional $k$-vector space $k[t]$.

(b) Over an affine open set $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq Y$, a closed immersion is $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\to\operatorname{Spec}A$. The $A$-module $A/I$ is cyclic, so the morphism is finite.

(c) Work over $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq Y$, with inverse image $\operatorname{Spec}B$. If $\mathcal F\cong\widetilde M$ there, then $M$ is finite over $B$, and $B$ is finite over $A$. Hence $M$ is finite over $A$, and $f_*\mathcal F$ is coherent.
