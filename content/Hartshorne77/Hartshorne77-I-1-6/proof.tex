Any two nonempty open subsets of an irreducible space intersect. Hence a nonempty open subset $U\subseteq X$ meets every nonempty open subset of $X$ and is dense. Two nonempty open subsets of $U$ are also open in $X$, so they intersect; thus $U$ is irreducible.

If $Y$ is irreducible and $\overline Y=F\cup G$ with $F,G$ closed, then $Y=(Y\cap F)\cup(Y\cap G)$, so $Y\subseteq F$ or $Y\subseteq G$. Taking closures gives $\overline Y=F$ or $\overline Y=G$.
