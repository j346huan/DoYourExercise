Write $\mathfrak a=(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$ and intersect successively with $V(f_i)$. By \exref{I.1.8}, each nonempty irreducible component either retains its dimension or has all components of its intersection of dimension one less. Every component of the resulting finite union is a component of one of these intersections. Starting with $\mathbb A^n$ gives dimension at least $n-r$.
