(a) On $w\ne0$, normalize $w=1$. The equations give $y=x^2,z=x^3$, so this part is the affine twisted cubic. Its projective closure is the twisted cubic parametrized by $(x,y,z,w)=(s^2t,st^2,t^3,s^3)$. On $w=0$, the equations give $x=0$, leaving the line $Z(x,w)$. Thus the intersection is the union of that cubic and the line.

(b) Setting $y=0$ in $x^2-yz=0$ gives $P=(0:0:1)$. But $I(C)+I(L)=(x^2-yz,y)=(x^2,y)$, whereas $I(P)=(x,y)$, so the ideals differ.
