(a) A descending chain of closed subsets gives an ascending chain of their homogeneous ideals, which stabilizes because $S$ is noetherian. The closed sets then stabilize by \exref{I.2.3}.

(b) If some closed set has no finite irreducible decomposition, choose a minimal one. It is reducible, so it is a union of two smaller closed sets, both having finite decompositions, a contradiction. Remove components contained in others. For uniqueness, an irreducible component in one decomposition is contained in a component of the other; applying this in both directions and using noncontainment shows that the components coincide.
