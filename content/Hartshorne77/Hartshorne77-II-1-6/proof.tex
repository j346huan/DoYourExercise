(a) The stalk of the quotient sheaf is canonically $\mathscr F_P/\mathscr F'_P$: a local coset maps to the coset of its germ, and a zero germ means that the section belongs to $\mathscr F'$ after shrinking. Thus the sequence of stalks is
\[0\to\mathscr F'_P\to\mathscr F_P\to\mathscr F_P/\mathscr F'_P\to0.\]
By \exref{II.1.2}, the sheaf sequence is exact.

(b) The first map identifies $\mathscr F'$ with its image subsheaf. The map $\mathscr F\to\mathscr F''$ vanishes on this subsheaf, so it factors through a morphism $\mathscr F/\mathscr F'\to\mathscr F''$. On every stalk this is the usual isomorphism from the quotient by the kernel onto the image. Hence it is an isomorphism of sheaves.
