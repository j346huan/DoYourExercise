Let $u$ be a uniformizer and $K=\operatorname{Frac}R$. The ring $R[t]$ has dimension $2$, while $R[t]/(ut-1)\cong R[1/u]=K$. Thus $P=(ut-1)$ is a closed point. Its height is $1$ by the principal ideal theorem, since it is a nonzero principal prime in a domain. Consequently $\dim\mathcal O_{X,P}=1\ne2=\dim X$, disproving (a).

For $Y=\{P\}$, we have $\dim Y=0$ and $\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=1$, so their sum is not $\dim X$, disproving (d). Finally, the nonempty open $D(u)=\operatorname{Spec}K[t]$ has dimension $1$, disproving (e).
