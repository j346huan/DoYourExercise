Assume $n\geq1$, and write $p:X\times\mathbb P^n\to X$. The standard affine charts are $X\times\mathbb A^n$, so repeated application of (6.6) shows that $X\times\mathbb P^n$ satisfies $(*)$. Put $H=X\times V_+(x_0)$. By (6.5) and (6.6), restriction gives an exact sequence $\mathbb Z[H]\to\operatorname{Cl}(X\times\mathbb P^n)\to\operatorname{Cl}X\to0$, split by $p^*$, where $p^*[Y]=[Y\times\mathbb P^n]$. Restriction to the generic fiber $\mathbb P^n_{K(X)}$, followed by degree, vanishes on $p^*\operatorname{Cl}X$ and sends $[H]$ to $1$. Thus $(D,m)\mapsto p^*D+m[H]$ is an isomorphism $\operatorname{Cl}X\oplus\mathbb Z\cong\operatorname{Cl}(X\times\mathbb P^n)$.

For $n=0$, the product is $X$ and the additional summand $\mathbb Z$ is absent.
