Since $\operatorname{Spec}k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ has one point, its $k$-morphisms to $X$ correspond to local $k$-algebra homomorphisms $\mathcal O_{X,x}\to k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$. The induced map $k(x)\to k$ is a $k$-algebra inclusion, so $k(x)=k$. Such a homomorphism kills $\mathfrak m_x^2$, and the structural map from $k$ gives $\mathcal O_{X,x}/\mathfrak m_x^2=k\oplus\mathfrak m_x/\mathfrak m_x^2$. It therefore has the form $c+\bar a\mapsto c+v(\bar a)\epsilon$, where $v:\mathfrak m_x/\mathfrak m_x^2\to k$ is $k$-linear. Conversely, every such $v$ defines a local $k$-algebra homomorphism, since both summands $\mathfrak m_x/\mathfrak m_x^2$ and $(\epsilon)$ have square zero. Thus the required datum is precisely a rational point $x$ and an element of $T_x$.
