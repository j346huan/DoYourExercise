(a) Lift a basis of $\mathcal F_x$ to finitely many local sections. After shrinking, they define $\mathcal O_U^r\to\mathcal F|_U$, whose kernel and cokernel are coherent and have zero stalk at $x$. Their supports are closed by \exref{II.5.6}, so shrinking again makes this map an isomorphism.

(b) One direction follows by taking stalks, and the other follows from (a).

(c) If $\mathcal F$ is invertible, evaluation gives $\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal F^\vee\cong\mathcal O_X$. Conversely, suppose $\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal G\cong\mathcal O_X$. At a point $x$, reducing modulo the maximal ideal shows that the product of the dimensions of the fibers of $\mathcal F$ and $\mathcal G$ is $1$. Nakayama's lemma therefore makes $\mathcal F_x$ cyclic, say $\mathcal F_x\cong\mathcal O_{X,x}/I$. Since $I$ annihilates $\mathcal F_x\otimes\mathcal G_x\cong\mathcal O_{X,x}$, we have $I=0$. Part (a) now shows that $\mathcal F$ is locally free of rank $1$.
