(a) The natural morphism $\mathscr F\to\operatorname{im}\varphi$ is surjective on stalks and has kernel $\ker\varphi$. Apply \exref{II.1.6} to obtain $\operatorname{im}\varphi\cong\mathscr F/\ker\varphi$.

(b) The projection from $\mathscr G$ to the sheafification of its presheaf cokernel is surjective on stalks, with kernel $\operatorname{im}\varphi$. The same result gives $\operatorname{coker}\varphi\cong\mathscr G/\operatorname{im}\varphi$.
