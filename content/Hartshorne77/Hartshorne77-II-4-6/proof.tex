Write $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $Y=\operatorname{Spec}B$, and regard the image of $B$ as a subring of $K=\operatorname{Frac}(A)$. For every valuation ring $R\subseteq K$ containing that image, the valuative criterion gives $\operatorname{Spec}R\to X$ extending the generic-point map. On rings this identifies $A$ with a subring of $R$. Thus $A$ lies in the intersection of all such valuation rings, which is the integral closure of the image of $B$ by Theorem 4.11A. Since $A$ is a finite-type $B$-algebra and is integral over $B$, it is a finite $B$-module. Hence $f$ is finite.
