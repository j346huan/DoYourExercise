The injection $\mathscr F'\to\mathscr F$ is injective on sections. If $s\in\mathscr F(U)$ maps to zero in $\mathscr F''(U)$, exactness on stalks supplies local lifts of $s$ to $\mathscr F'$. Any two lifts agree on overlaps because the map $\mathscr F'\to\mathscr F$ is injective on sections. They therefore glue to a section of $\mathscr F'(U)$ mapping to $s$. This proves exactness at $\mathscr F(U)$ and hence left exactness of $\Gamma(U,\cdot)$.
