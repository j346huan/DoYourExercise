(a) The domain is $D_+(x_0)$, where $f=x_1/x_0$ is the usual affine coordinate. At a point with $x_0=0,x_1\ne0$, its reciprocal $x_0/x_1$ lies in the maximal ideal, so $f$ is not regular. At $(0:0:1)$, it is $u/v$ in $k[u,v]_{(u,v)}$, with $u=x_1/x_2,v=x_0/x_2$, and $v$ does not divide $u$. Thus it is not regular there either.

(b) The map is $(x_0:x_1:x_2)\mapsto(x_0:x_1)$, defined on $\mathbb P^2\setminus\{(0:0:1)\}$. It cannot extend at the omitted point: on $x_0=0$ it is constantly $(0:1)$ away from that point, whereas on $x_1=0$ it is constantly $(1:0)$. Continuity of an extension would give both values at the omitted point.
