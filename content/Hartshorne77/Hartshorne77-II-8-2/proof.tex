Since $X$ is quasi-compact, finitely many elements of $V$ generate $\mathcal E$. Replacing $V$ by their span, assume $\dim_k V=N$, and write $r=\operatorname{rank}\mathcal E$. The evaluation map $V\otimes_k\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal E$ is surjective and locally split. Its kernel is locally free of rank $N-r$.

The closed incidence subset $Z=\{(x,s)\in X\times V:s_x=0\text{ in }\mathcal E\otimes k(x)\}$ is the total space of this kernel. Hence $\dim Z=n+N-r<N$. The closure of its image in $V$ therefore has dimension less than $N$. Choose $s\in V$ outside this closure. Then $s_x\notin\mathfrak m_x\mathcal E_x$ for every $x$.

In a local basis of $\mathcal E$, one coordinate of $s$ is a unit near each point. Thus $\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal E$, $1\mapsto s$, is locally a split inclusion of a free summand. It is injective, and its cokernel $\mathcal E'$ is locally free of rank $r-1$.
