(a) Choose coordinates with $P=(1:0:\cdots:0)$ and the hyperplane $x_0=0$. The line through $P$ and $Q=(a_0:\cdots:a_{n+1})$ meets the hyperplane at $(0:a_1:\cdots:a_{n+1})$. Thus the projection is $(x_0:\cdots:x_{n+1})\mapsto(x_1:\cdots:x_{n+1})$, which is regular away from $P$ by its coordinate ratios.

(b) The image is parametrized by $(x:y:w)=(t^3:t^2u:u^3)$, so it lies in $Z(y^3-x^2w)$. Conversely, if $x\ne0$, normalize $x=1$; then $w=y^3$ and the point comes from $(t:u)=(1:y)$. If $x=0$, then $y=0$, giving the image of $(0:1)$. Hence the image is exactly this cubic. In the chart $w=1$, its equation is $x^2=y^3$, with its cusp at $(0:0:1)$.
