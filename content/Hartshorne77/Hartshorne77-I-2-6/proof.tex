For each nonempty $Y_i=Y\cap U_i$, dehomogenization identifies $A(Y_i)$ with the degree-zero subring of $S(Y)_{x_i}$. The grading gives $S(Y)_{x_i}\cong A(Y_i)[x_i,x_i^{-1}]$: a homogeneous element of degree $d$ is $x_i^d$ times a degree-zero element, and distinct degrees cannot cancel. Taking fraction fields and transcendence degrees gives $\dim S(Y)=\dim Y_i+1$. Thus all nonempty $Y_i$ have the same dimension, and \exref{I.1.10}(b) gives $\dim Y=\dim S(Y)-1$.
