(a) By \exref{I.2.8}, write the curves as $Z(f)$ and $Z(g)$. Their affine cones intersect in $V(f,g)\subseteq\mathbb A^3$, all of whose components have dimension at least $1$ by \exref{I.1.9}. Thus the intersection contains a nonzero point, giving a projective intersection point.

(b) If $Y\cap H=\varnothing$, then $Y$ is closed in the affine variety $\mathbb P^n\setminus H$ by \exref{I.3.5}. Hence $Y$ is both affine and projective, so it is a point by \exref{I.3.1}(e), contradicting $\dim Y\ge1$.
