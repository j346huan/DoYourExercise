Write $H=V(f)$. If $Y\cap H=\varnothing$, there is nothing to prove. Otherwise the image of $f$ in the domain $A(Y)$ is nonzero and not a unit. Each minimal prime $\mathfrak p$ over $(f)$ has height $1$ by the principal ideal theorem. The corresponding component has dimension $\dim A(Y)/\mathfrak p=\dim A(Y)-1=r-1$ by (1.8A).
