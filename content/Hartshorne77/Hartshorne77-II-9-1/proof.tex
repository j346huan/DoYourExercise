(a) The conormal sequence (8.17) and the Euler sequence (8.13), restricted to $Y$, give inclusions $\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2\to\Omega_X|_Y\to\mathcal O_Y(-1)^{n+1}$. Both cokernels are locally free, so these inclusions split locally.

(b) By (8.21A(e)), $\mathcal I^r/\mathcal I^{r+1}\cong\operatorname{Sym}^r(\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2)$. Thus (a) embeds it in a direct sum of copies of $\mathcal O_Y(-r)$. Choose a hyperplane meeting $Y$ but not containing it, with equation $h$. For $s\in\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y(-r))$, the section $h^rs$ is a regular function on the projective integral variety $Y$, hence constant by (I, 3.4(a)). It vanishes on the hyperplane section, so it is zero. Since $h\ne0$, also $s=0$.

(c) The assertion for $r=1$ is (I, 3.4(a)). The exact sequence $0\to\mathcal I^r/\mathcal I^{r+1}\to\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I^{r+1}\to\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I^r\to0$ and (b) show that the map on global sections is injective. Its image contains the constants, so induction gives $\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I^r)=k$ for every $r\ge1$.

(d) By (9.2), $\Gamma(\widehat X,\mathcal O_{\widehat X})=\varprojlim_r\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I^r)=k$.
