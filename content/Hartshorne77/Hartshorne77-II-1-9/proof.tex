Both the uniqueness and existence of gluing hold coordinatewise, so $U\mapsto\mathscr F(U)\oplus\mathscr G(U)$ is a sheaf. The coordinate inclusions and projections are morphisms of sheaves. Given maps $\mathscr F\to\mathscr H$, $\mathscr G\to\mathscr H$, their sum defines the unique morphism $\mathscr F\oplus\mathscr G\to\mathscr H$ with those restrictions. Given maps $\mathscr H\to\mathscr F$, $\mathscr H\to\mathscr G$, pairing them defines the unique map to $\mathscr F\oplus\mathscr G$ with those projections. Thus it is both the categorical direct sum and direct product.
