Write the blowup as $xu=yt$ in $\mathbb A^2\times\mathbb P^1$. On $t=1$, substitute $y=xu$ into $y^2=x^3$. Removing the exceptional factor $x^2$ gives the strict transform $x=u^2,y=u^3$, isomorphic to $\mathbb A^1$. On $u=1$, its equation is $t^3y=1$, so this chart adds no points outside $t\ne0$. Thus the first chart contains the whole strict transform.

It meets $E$ only at $u=0$, namely $((0,0),(1:0))$. The induced map is $u\mapsto(u^2,u^3)$, which is bijective and bicontinuous but not an isomorphism by \exref{I.3.2}(a).
