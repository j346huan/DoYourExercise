The assertion is local, so assume $\mathfrak X$ is affine. By (9.7), $\mathcal F'$ and $\mathcal F''$ have finite sets of global generators. Lift generators of $\mathcal F''$ to $\mathcal F$ using \exref{II.9.3}, and adjoin generators of $\mathcal F'$. This gives a surjection $\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X}^r\to\mathcal F$. Let $\mathcal K''$ be the kernel of the composite map to $\mathcal F''$. It is coherent by (9.9). The kernel $\mathcal K$ of $\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X}^r\to\mathcal F$ is also the kernel of $\mathcal K''\to\mathcal F'$, hence coherent by (9.9). Therefore $\mathcal F\cong\operatorname{coker}(\mathcal K\to\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X}^r)$ is coherent.
