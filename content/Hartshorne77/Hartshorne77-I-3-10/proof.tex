The restricted map is continuous for the induced topologies. A regular function on $Y'$ is locally the restriction of a quotient of ambient polynomials, with denominator nonzero near the point in question. The same quotient defines a regular function on a neighborhood in $Y$. Its pullback by $\varphi$ is regular, and restricting to $X'$ preserves regularity. Hence $\varphi|_{X'}:X'\to Y'$ is a morphism.
