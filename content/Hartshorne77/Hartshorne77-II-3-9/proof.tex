(a) The affine product is $\operatorname{Spec}(k[x]\otimes_kk[y])\cong\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y]=\mathbb A_k^2$. The distinct points $(0)$ and $(x-y)$ both map to the pair $((0),(0))$ in the two factors. Thus the natural map of underlying sets to the product set is not injective.

(b) The product is $\operatorname{Spec}(k(s)\otimes_kk(t))$, where
\[k(s)\otimes_kk(t)\cong S^{-1}k[s,t],\qquad S=\{g(s)h(t):0\ne g\in k[s],\ 0\ne h\in k[t]\}.\]
Its points are $(0)$ and the primes $(F)$ for irreducible $F\in k[s,t]$ having positive degree in both variables. Indeed, primes meeting $S$ are precisely those with nonzero contraction to $k[s]$ or $k[t]$, and every maximal ideal of $k[s,t]$ has both contractions nonzero by Zariski's lemma. The remaining nonzero primes have height one and the stated form. They are closed points of the product; $(0)$ is generic. Every proper closed subset is finite, since a nonzero polynomial has only finitely many irreducible factors. The residue field at $(F)$ is $\operatorname{Frac}(k[s,t]/(F))$, and the residue field at $(0)$ is $k(s,t)$.
