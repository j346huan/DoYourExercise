Let $\overline X$ be the closure of $X$ in $Y$. Since $X$ is locally closed, it is open in the curve $\overline X$, and they have the same function field $K$. For any $Q\in\overline X$, choose a discrete valuation ring of $K/k$ dominating $\mathcal O_{Q,\overline X}$, by localizing its finite integral closure at a maximal ideal. Since $X$ is nonsingular and projective, every such valuation ring is $\mathcal O_{P,X}$ for some $P\in X$. As $X$ is open in $\overline X$, this is also $\mathcal O_{P,\overline X}$. A valuation ring has at most one center on a variety by Proposition 6.4, so $Q=P\in X$. Thus $X=\overline X$ is closed in $Y$.
