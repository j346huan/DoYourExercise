Add morphisms sectionwise: $(\varphi+\psi)(V)(s)=\varphi(V)(s)+\psi(V)(s)$. This respects restrictions and gives $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathscr F|_U,\mathscr G|_U)$ an abelian group structure.

Suppose morphisms $\varphi_i$ are given on an open cover $\{U_i\}$ of $U$ and agree on overlaps. For $V\subset U$ and $s\in\mathscr F(V)$, the sections $\varphi_i(V\cap U_i)(s|_{V\cap U_i})$ agree on overlaps, so glue uniquely to a section $\varphi(V)(s)\in\mathscr G(V)$. Additivity and compatibility with restrictions hold locally, hence globally. Thus $\varphi$ is a morphism restricting to each $\varphi_i$, and locality in $\mathscr G$ makes it unique. This proves the sheaf axioms.
