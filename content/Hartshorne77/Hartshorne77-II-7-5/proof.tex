(a) For every coherent $\mathcal F$ and all sufficiently large $n$, both $\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal L^n$ and $\mathcal M^n$ are generated by global sections. Their tensor product is therefore generated, proving that $\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal M$ is ample.

(b) For all sufficiently large $n$, $\mathcal M\otimes\mathcal L^{n-1}$ is generated by global sections. Apply (a) to $\mathcal L$ and this sheaf.

(c) Choose $n>0$ such that $\mathcal M^n$ is generated by global sections. Then $\mathcal L^n\otimes\mathcal M^n$ is ample by (a), so $\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal M$ is ample by (7.5).

(d) Choose an immersion $i:X\to\mathbb P_A^r$ inducing $\mathcal L$. Finitely many global sections generating $\mathcal M$ define $j:X\to\mathbb P_A^s$. The map $(i,j)$ is an immersion: it is the graph of $j$, followed by the base change of $i$. Composing with the Segre embedding gives an immersion inducing $\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal M$.

(e) By (7.6), $\mathcal L^r$ is very ample for some $r>0$. For all sufficiently large $n$, $\mathcal L^{n-r}$ is generated by global sections, so (d) makes $\mathcal L^n$ very ample.
