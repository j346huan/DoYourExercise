(a) Suppose a germ $s_P$ maps to zero. After shrinking a representative to some neighborhood $V$, its image in $\mathscr G(V)$ is zero. Injectivity of $\varphi(V)$ then makes the representative zero, so the map on stalks is injective. Sheafification preserves stalks, and \exref{II.1.2} therefore shows that $\varphi^+$ is injective.

(b) The image presheaf embeds sectionwise in $\mathscr G$. Applying (a) and using $\mathscr G^+=\mathscr G$ gives the natural injection $\operatorname{im}\varphi\to\mathscr G$.
