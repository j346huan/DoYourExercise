(a) Choose $Y$-immersions $i:X\to\mathbb P_Y^r$ and $j:X\to\mathbb P_Y^s$ inducing $\mathcal L$ and $\mathcal M$. The map $(i,j)$ is an immersion: factor it through the graph $X\to X\times_Y\mathbb P_Y^s$, a closed immersion, followed by the base change of $i$. Composing with the Segre embedding gives an immersion into $\mathbb P_Y^{rs+r+s}$. By \exref{II.5.11}, the pullback of $\mathcal O(1)$ is $\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal M$.

(b) Choose immersions $i:X\to\mathbb P_Y^r$ and $j:Y\to\mathbb P_Z^s$ inducing $\mathcal L$ and $\mathcal M$. The composite
\[X\to\mathbb P_Y^r=\mathbb P_Z^r\times_ZY\to\mathbb P_Z^r\times_Z\mathbb P_Z^s\to\mathbb P_Z^{rs+r+s}\]
is an immersion, since the middle map is a base change of $j$ and the last is the Segre embedding. Its pullback of $\mathcal O(1)$ is $\mathcal L\otimes f^*\mathcal M$.
