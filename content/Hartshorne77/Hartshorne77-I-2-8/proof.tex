By \exref{I.2.6} and (1.8A), $\dim Y=n-1$ exactly when $I(Y)$ has height $1$. Since $S$ is a UFD, this holds exactly when $I(Y)=(f)$ for an irreducible polynomial $f$. A generator of a homogeneous principal ideal may be taken homogeneous, and its degree is positive. Conversely, such an $f$ generates a homogeneous prime ideal of height $1$, giving $\dim Z(f)=n-1$.
