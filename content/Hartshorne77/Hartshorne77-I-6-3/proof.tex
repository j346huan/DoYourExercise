(a) The morphism $\mathbb{A}^2-\{0\}\to\mathbb{P}^1$, $(x,y)\mapsto(x:y)$, cannot extend over the origin. On the punctured $x$-axis it is constantly $(1:0)$, so continuity would force that value at the origin; the punctured $y$-axis similarly forces $(0:1)$.

(b) The morphism $\mathbb{A}^1-\{0\}\to\mathbb{A}^1$, $x\mapsto1/x$, cannot extend over the origin, since $1/x\notin k[x]_{(x)}$. Here the source is nonsingular and the target is not projective.
