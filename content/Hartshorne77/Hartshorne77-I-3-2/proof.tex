(a) The map $t\mapsto(t^2,t^3)$ is bijective onto $Y=V(y^2-x^3)$: away from the origin its inverse is $y/x$, and the origin has the unique preimage $0$. Proper closed subsets of either curve are finite, so the bijection is bicontinuous. Its pullback is the inclusion $k[t^2,t^3]\to k[t]$, which is not surjective since $t\notin k[t^2,t^3]$. Thus it is not an isomorphism.

(b) The map $t\mapsto t^p$ is injective because $a^p-b^p=(a-b)^p$, and surjective because $k$ is algebraically closed. Again it is bicontinuous for the cofinite topology. Its pullback $k[t]\to k[t]$, $t\mapsto t^p$, is not surjective, so it is not an isomorphism.
