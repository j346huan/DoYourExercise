Given $\varphi:A\to\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$, choose an affine open cover $U_i=\operatorname{Spec}B_i$. The maps $A\to\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)\to B_i$ induce morphisms $f_i:U_i\to\operatorname{Spec}A$. On every affine open $W\subseteq U_i\cap U_j$, both restrictions are induced by $A\to\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)\to\Gamma(W,\mathcal O_X)$, so they agree. The $f_i$ glue to a morphism $f$.

The map on global sections induced by $f$ restricts on every $U_i$ to the restriction of $\varphi$, so it equals $\varphi$. Conversely, a morphism $X\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ restricts on each $U_i$ to the affine morphism induced by its map on global sections. Thus these constructions are inverse.
