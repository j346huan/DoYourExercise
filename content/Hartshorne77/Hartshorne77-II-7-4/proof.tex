(a) By (7.6), some positive power of the ample sheaf gives an immersion $X\to\mathbb P_A^n$. Since immersions and $\mathbb P_A^n\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ are separated, so is $X\to\operatorname{Spec}A$.

(b) Write $X=U\cup V$, where $U=V=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]$ and $U\cap V=\operatorname{Spec}k[t,t^{-1}]$. Every invertible sheaf is trivial on $U$ and $V$. Its transition function is $ct^r$, with $c\in k^*$ and $r\in\mathbb Z$, and changes of trivializations remove exactly $c$. Therefore $\operatorname{Pic}X\cong\mathbb Z$.

For the sheaf with transition $t^r$, global sections are pairs $(f,g)\in k[t]^2$ satisfying $f=t^rg$. If $r>0$, every section vanishes at the origin of $U$; if $r<0$, every section vanishes at the origin of $V$. Hence only $\mathcal O_X$ is generated by global sections. An ample invertible sheaf has all sufficiently large powers generated, so its class must be zero. But $\mathcal O_X$ is not ample, since tensoring a nontrivial invertible sheaf with its powers never makes that sheaf generated by global sections. Thus $X$ has no ample invertible sheaf.
