(a) The homogeneous coordinate ring of the $d$-uple image has degree $m$ part $k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]_{dm}$. Thus its Hilbert polynomial is $\binom{dz+n}{n}$, with leading coefficient $d^n/n!$. Its dimension is $n$, so its degree is $d^n$.

(b) The degree $m$ part of the coordinate ring of the Segre image is $k[x_0,\ldots,x_r]_m\otimes_k k[y_0,\ldots,y_s]_m$. Its Hilbert polynomial is $\binom{z+r}{r}\binom{z+s}{s}$, with leading coefficient $1/(r!s!)$. The image has dimension $r+s$, so its degree is $\binom{r+s}{r}$.
