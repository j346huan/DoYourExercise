Take $f=(x^2-1)^2+y^2$. Over $\mathbb C$, its irreducible factors are $y+i(x^2-1)$ and $y-i(x^2-1)$, which are interchanged by conjugation and are not associates. Thus no proper nonconstant factor is real, so $f$ is irreducible in $\mathbb R[x,y]$. Its real zero set is $\{(1,0),(-1,0)\}$, which is reducible.
