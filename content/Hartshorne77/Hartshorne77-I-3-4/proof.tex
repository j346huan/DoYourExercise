The map $\rho_d$ is a morphism because on each standard affine chart its coordinate ratios are regular. On the image chart where the coordinate for $x_i^d$ is nonzero, its inverse has coordinate functions $x_j/x_i=y_{x_i^{d-1}x_j}/y_{x_i^d}$. These are regular and agree on overlaps, so the inverse is a morphism.
