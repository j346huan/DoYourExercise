(a) On an open set with basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$, a basis of $T^r\mathcal F$ consists of all ordered tensors of $r$ basis elements. A basis of $S^r\mathcal F$ consists of the monomials of total degree $r$, and a basis of $\bigwedge^r\mathcal F$ consists of $e_{i_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge e_{i_r}$ with $i_1<\cdots<i_r$. Their ranks are $n^r$, $\binom{n+r-1}{r}$, and $\binom nr$.

(b) The map $\bigwedge^r\mathcal F\to\mathcal Hom(\bigwedge^{n-r}\mathcal F,\bigwedge^n\mathcal F)$ sends $u$ to $(v\mapsto u\wedge v)$. In the bases from (a), a basis wedge pairs nontrivially only with its complementary wedge, with coefficient $1$ or $-1$. Thus the map is an isomorphism. Apply \exref{II.5.1}(b), and take $r=1,n=2$ for the stated special case.

(c) Define $F^p$ intrinsically as the image of $S^p\mathcal F'\otimes S^{r-p}\mathcal F\to S^r\mathcal F$. The sequence splits locally because $\mathcal F''$ is locally free. Under a local splitting,
\[F^p=\bigoplus_{i\ge p}S^i\mathcal F'\otimes S^{r-i}\mathcal F''.\]
It follows that $F^0=S^r\mathcal F$, $F^{r+1}=0$, and $F^p/F^{p+1}\cong S^p\mathcal F'\otimes S^{r-p}\mathcal F''$. The quotient map is independent of the splitting: replacing a lift from $\mathcal F''$ changes the product by an element of $F^{p+1}$.

(d) Define $F^p$ as the image of $\bigwedge^p\mathcal F'\otimes\bigwedge^{r-p}\mathcal F\to\bigwedge^r\mathcal F$. The same local splitting and independence argument give the exterior-power filtration. For $r=n'+n''$, the only nonzero quotient occurs at $p=n'$, giving $\bigwedge^n\mathcal F\cong\bigwedge^{n'}\mathcal F'\otimes\bigwedge^{n''}\mathcal F''$.

(e) Pullback commutes with direct sums, tensor products, and cokernels. The canonical tensor-product isomorphisms therefore give $f^*T(\mathcal F)\cong T(f^*\mathcal F)$. Under this isomorphism, the ideals generated by $u\otimes v-v\otimes u$, or by $u\otimes u$, generate the corresponding ideals after extension of scalars. Taking the quotients gives the assertions for symmetric and exterior algebras, and then for their graded components.
