It suffices to work on an affine open subset, with coordinate ring $A$. Let $\overline A$ be its integral closure in its fraction field. By Theorem 3.9A, choose finitely many $A$-module generators $b_1,\ldots,b_m$ of $\overline A$. Since integral closure commutes with localization, $A_{\mathfrak m_P}$ is normal exactly when every $b_i$ belongs to $A_{\mathfrak m_P}$. The regularity locus of each rational function $b_i$ is open, so the normal locus is open. It is nonempty: a common nonzero denominator $s\in A$ of the $b_i$ gives $\overline A_s=A_s$. These descriptions agree on overlapping affine opens, so the nonnormal locus is a proper closed subset of the variety.
