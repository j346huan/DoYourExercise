The inverse image of $Z$ under the Veronese homeomorphism is a curve in $\mathbb P^2$, hence $Z(f)$ for an irreducible homogeneous $f$. Every monomial of $f^2$ has even degree and is a product of quadratic monomials, so $f^2=\theta(g)$ for a homogeneous $g$ in the coordinates of $\mathbb P^5$. Thus $Z(g)\cap Y=Z$. Some irreducible factor $h$ of $g$ vanishes on $Z$, since $Z$ is irreducible. As $Z(h)\subseteq Z(g)$, we have $Z(h)\cap Y=Z$. Take $V=Z(h)$.
