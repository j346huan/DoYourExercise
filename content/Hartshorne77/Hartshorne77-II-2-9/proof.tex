Choose an affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ meeting $Z$. Write $Z\cap U=V(I)$. Its irreducibility implies that $\sqrt I$ is prime: if $ab\in\sqrt I$, then $V(I)\subseteq V(a)\cup V(b)$, so $a$ or $b$ belongs to $\sqrt I$. Thus $\sqrt I$ is the generic point of $Z\cap U$. Since $Z\cap U$ is a nonempty open subset of the irreducible space $Z$, it is dense in $Z$, and this point is generic for $Z$.

Every generic point of $Z$ belongs to $U$, since otherwise its closure would be contained in $X\setminus U$. Its closure in $U$ is $V(I)$, whose generic point is uniquely $\sqrt I$.
