(a) Every conic is isomorphic to $\mathbb P^1$ by \exref{I.3.1}(c).

(b) The cusp has parametrization $(x,y)=(t^2,t^3)$, with rational inverse $t=y/x$. Thus its function field is $k(t)$.

(c) Projection is $(x:y:z)\mapsto(x:y)$. For $(s:t)\in\mathbb P^1$, its rational inverse is
\[(s:t)\mapsto\bigl(s(t^2-s^2):t(t^2-s^2):s^3\bigr).\]
Substitution gives $y^2z=x^2(x+z)$, and the compositions are identities on the nonempty open sets where $s(t^2-s^2)\ne0$ and $x(y^2-x^2)\ne0$, respectively. Thus the map is birational.
