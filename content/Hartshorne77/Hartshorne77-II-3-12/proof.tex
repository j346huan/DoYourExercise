(a) Since $\varphi(S_+)=T_+$, the open set in \exref{II.2.14}(b) is all of $\operatorname{Proj}T$. Contraction identifies its points homeomorphically with $V_+(\ker\varphi)$. For homogeneous $f\in S_+$, the induced map $S_{(f)}\to T_{(\varphi(f))}$ is surjective, because homogeneous numerators lift in the same degree. Thus the morphism is a closed immersion on each $D_+(f)$ and hence globally.

(b) The inclusion $I'\subseteq I$ gives $S/I'\to S/I$, an isomorphism in every degree at least $d_0$. By \exref{II.2.14}(c), it induces an isomorphism $\operatorname{Proj}(S/I)\to\operatorname{Proj}(S/I')$ commuting with the closed immersions into $\operatorname{Proj}S$. Thus the two ideals define the same closed subscheme. They may differ: in $S=k[x_0,x_1]$, the distinct ideals $(x_0)$ and $(x_0^2,x_0x_1)$ have the same components in all degrees at least $2$.
