(a) It suffices to prove that $X$ is affine when $Y=\operatorname{Spec}A$ has an affine open covering with affine inverse images. Refining that covering, choose finitely many $D(a_i)$ covering $Y$, each contained in one of its members. Its inverse image is a distinguished open subset of an affine scheme, hence affine. The pullbacks of the $a_i$ generate the unit ideal in $\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$, so the affine criterion of \exref{II.2.17} makes $X$ affine. Applying this after restriction to any affine open in $Y$ proves the assertion.

(b) By (a), inverse images of affine opens are quasi-compact. Over affine opens the diagonal is induced by $B\otimes_AB\to B$, $b\otimes c\mapsto bc$, which is surjective. Thus $f$ is quasi-compact and separated. A finite morphism is affine by definition.

(c) For $V=\operatorname{Spec}A$, put $X_V=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal A(V)$. Quasi-coherence identifies $\mathcal A(D(a))$ with $\mathcal A(V)_a$, so distinguished opens in $V$ induce the corresponding open immersions in $X_V$. For any affine $U\subseteq V$, these maps glue to an isomorphism of $\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal A(U)$ with the inverse image of $U$ in $X_V$. They are induced by restriction. On intersections, the resulting identifications satisfy the cocycle condition, so the $X_V$ glue to $X\to Y$. The same local description gives uniqueness.

(d) The morphism in (c) is affine and has $f_*\mathcal O_X|_V=\widetilde{\mathcal A(V)}=\mathcal A|_V$, so $f_*\mathcal O_X\cong\mathcal A$. Conversely, for affine $f$, its direct image of the structure sheaf is quasi-coherent by the affine module calculation. On every affine $V$, one has $f^{-1}(V)=\operatorname{Spec}\Gamma(f^{-1}(V),\mathcal O_X)=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal A(V)$, compatibly with restriction. Part (c) gives $X\cong\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal A$.

(e) For a quasi-coherent $\mathcal A$-module $\mathcal M$, associate on $f^{-1}(V)=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal A(V)$ the sheaf of the $\mathcal A(V)$-module $\mathcal M(V)$. Localization makes these sheaves and their morphisms glue. This construction is inverse to $f_*$ on each affine open by the equivalence between modules and quasi-coherent sheaves, hence globally.
