(a) Under the embedding determined by $D$, $\mathcal L\cong\mathcal O_X(1)$. By \exref{II.5.14}(b), $\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X(n))$ agrees with the degree-$n$ part of the homogeneous coordinate ring for all sufficiently large $n$. Its dimension is $P_X(n)$ for such $n$, so $\dim|nD|=P_X(n)-1$.

(b) If $\mathcal L^n$ has a nonzero section $s$, then $s^{\otimes r}$ is a nonzero global section of $\mathcal O_X$, hence a nonzero constant. Thus $s$ is nowhere zero and $\mathcal L^n\cong\mathcal O_X$. This occurs exactly when $r\mid n$, and then $\dim\Gamma(X,\mathcal L^n)=1$. Consequently $\dim|nD|=0$ when $r\mid n$, and $-1$ otherwise.
