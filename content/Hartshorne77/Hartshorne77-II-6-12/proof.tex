Define $\deg\mathcal F=\deg(\det\mathcal F)$, using \exref{II.6.11}. Determinant is additive on exact sequences, and degree is a homomorphism $\operatorname{Pic}X\to\mathbb Z$ by (6.10), so this degree is additive. For an invertible sheaf $\mathcal L(D)$, its determinant is itself, hence $\deg\mathcal L(D)=\deg D$. For a torsion sheaf $\mathcal F$, a composition series at each point and \exref{II.6.11}(b) give
\[\det\mathcal F=\mathcal L\left(\sum_P\operatorname{length}(\mathcal F_P)P\right),\qquad \deg\mathcal F=\sum_P\operatorname{length}(\mathcal F_P).\]
Finally, \exref{II.6.11}(c) expresses the class of every coherent sheaf as a sum of classes of an invertible sheaf and torsion sheaves. Additivity and the prescribed values on these classes determine its degree uniquely.
