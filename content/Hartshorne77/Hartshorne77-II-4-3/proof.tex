The diagonal identifies $U\cap V$ with a closed subscheme of $U\times_SV$. Since $S,U,V$ are affine, their fiber product is affine, so $U\cap V$ is affine.

For a counterexample, glue two copies of $\mathbb A_k^2$ along $\mathbb A_k^2\setminus\{0\}$. Their intersection is not affine: its ring of global sections is $k[x,y]_x\cap k[x,y]_y=k[x,y]$ inside $k(x,y)$. If the intersection were affine, its inclusion into $\mathbb A_k^2$ would therefore be an isomorphism, contrary to the missing origin.
