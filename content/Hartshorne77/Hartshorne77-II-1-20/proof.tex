(a) A section $s\in\mathscr F(V)$ has support in $Z\cap V$ exactly when its restriction to $V-Z$ is zero: vanishing of all germs there is equivalent to vanishing of the section. Hence the given presheaf is the kernel of the restriction morphism $\mathscr F\to j_*(\mathscr F|_U)$. Kernels of sheaf morphisms are sheaves, so it is a sheaf.

(b) This kernel description gives the required exact sequence. If $\mathscr F$ is flasque, every restriction $\mathscr F(V)\to\mathscr F(V\cap U)$ is surjective. Thus the last morphism is surjective even on sections, and in particular is a surjective morphism of sheaves.
