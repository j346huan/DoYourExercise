(ii)$\Rightarrow$(iii): Choose $q_i>0$ with $x_i^{q_i}\in\mathfrak a$, and take $d=1+\sum_i(q_i-1)$. Every monomial of degree $d$ is divisible by some $x_i^{q_i}$, so $S_d\subseteq\mathfrak a$.

(iii)$\Rightarrow$(i): At any projective point some $x_i\ne0$, and then $x_i^d\in\mathfrak a$ does not vanish there.
