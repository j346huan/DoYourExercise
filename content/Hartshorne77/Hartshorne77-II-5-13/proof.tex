For $x\in S_1$, the corresponding charts satisfy $(S^{(d)})_{(x^d)}=S_{(x)}$: denominators in a degree-zero fraction can be increased until their exponents are divisible by $d$. These identifications commute with localization and cover both schemes, giving $\operatorname{Proj}S^{(d)}\cong\operatorname{Proj}S$.

On the same charts, $S^{(d)}(1)_{(x^d)}=S(d)_{(x)}$, since both consist of homogeneous fractions of degree $d$ in $S_x$. These compatible module identifications show that $\mathcal O(1)$ corresponds to $\mathcal O_X(d)$. If $S_1$ is generated by $x_0,\ldots,x_r$, its degree-$d$ monomials generate $S_d$, so the resulting embedding is the $d$-uple embedding.
