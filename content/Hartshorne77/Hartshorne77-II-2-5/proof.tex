The points are $(0)$ and $(p)$ for prime integers $p$. The proper closed subsets are the finite sets of points $(p)$; the closure of $(0)$ is the whole space. By \exref{II.2.4}, morphisms $X\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$ correspond to ring homomorphisms $\mathbb Z\to\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$, of which there is exactly one.
