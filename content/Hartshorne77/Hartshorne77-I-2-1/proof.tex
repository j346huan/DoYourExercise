Regard $\mathfrak a$ as an ideal in the affine coordinate ring of $\mathbb A^{n+1}$. The homogeneous polynomial $f$ vanishes at every nonzero affine zero of $\mathfrak a$, and also at the origin since $\deg f>0$. The affine Nullstellensatz gives $f\in\sqrt{\mathfrak a}$, hence $f^q\in\mathfrak a$ for some $q>0$.
