Restrict to a nonempty affine open $\operatorname{Spec}B\subseteq Y$ and choose a finite nonempty affine open cover $\operatorname{Spec}A_i$ of its inverse image. Dominance and integrality make every $B\to A_i$ injective. Each $A_i\otimes_BK(Y)$ is a finitely generated domain over $K(Y)$ with finite spectrum. By Noether normalization, its dimension is zero, since a polynomial ring in at least one variable over a field has infinitely many primes. It is therefore a finite field extension of $K(Y)$, and equals $K(X)$.

Clear the denominators of monic equations over $K(Y)$ for finite sets of $B$-algebra generators of all the $A_i$. After inverting one nonzero element of $B$, every $A_i$ is finite over $B$. Choose a nonempty affine open $W$ contained in all the $\operatorname{Spec}A_i$. Their generic fibers consist only of the generic point of $X$, which belongs to $W$. For each $i$, the closed subset $\operatorname{Spec}A_i\setminus W$ therefore has closed image in $\operatorname{Spec}B$ by \exref{II.3.5}(b), and that image avoids the generic point. Remove the union of these finitely many images and choose a nonempty distinguished open $U=D(b)$ in the complement. Then $f^{-1}(U)\subseteq W$, so it equals the inverse image of $U$ in any one $\operatorname{Spec}A_i$. It is affine and finite over $U$.
