The Segre morphism $\mathbb P_Z^r\times_Z\mathbb P_Z^s\to\mathbb P_Z^{rs+r+s}$ is a closed immersion. Indeed, its image is defined by $z_{ij}z_{k\ell}-z_{i\ell}z_{kj}$, and on $D_+(z_{ij})$ these equations identify its coordinate ring with that of $D_+(x_i)\times_ZD_+(y_j)$ via $z_{k\ell}/z_{ij}\mapsto(x_k/x_i)(y_\ell/y_j)$.

Given closed immersions $X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P_Y^r$ and $Y\hookrightarrow\mathbb P_Z^s$, compose
\[X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P_Y^r=\mathbb P_Z^r\times_ZY\hookrightarrow\mathbb P_Z^r\times_Z\mathbb P_Z^s\hookrightarrow\mathbb P_Z^{rs+r+s}.\]
Every map is a closed immersion, so $X\to Z$ is projective. Closed immersions are projective using $\mathbb P_Y^0=Y$, and base change preserves the defining closed immersion into projective space. Thus projective morphisms satisfy (a)--(c) of \exref{II.4.8}, and hence (d)--(f).
