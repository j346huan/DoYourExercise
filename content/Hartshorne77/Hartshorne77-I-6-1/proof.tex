(a) By Proposition 6.7 and Theorem 6.9, $Y$ is isomorphic to an open subset of the nonsingular projective curve with function field $k(t)$, namely $\mathbb{P}^1$. Since this open subset is proper, move a missing point to infinity by a projective linear transformation. Then $Y$ is isomorphic to an open subset of $\mathbb{A}^1$.

(b) Write that open subset as $\mathbb{A}^1-\{a_1,\ldots,a_m\}$. It is isomorphic to the affine curve $\prod_i(x-a_i)y=1$ by projection onto $x$, with inverse $x\mapsto(x,\prod_i(x-a_i)^{-1})$.

(c) Its coordinate ring is $k[x,\prod_i(x-a_i)^{-1}]$, a localization of the unique factorization domain $k[x]$. Therefore $A(Y)$ is a unique factorization domain.
