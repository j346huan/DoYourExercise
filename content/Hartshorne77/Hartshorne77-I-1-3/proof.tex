\[V(x^2-yz,xz-x)=V(x,y)\cup V(x,z)\cup V(z-1,y-x^2).\]
The ideals $(x,y)$, $(x,z)$, and $(z-1,y-x^2)$ have quotient rings $k[z]$, $k[y]$, and $k[x]$, respectively, so they are prime. None of the corresponding closed sets contains another. Thus these are exactly the irreducible components and their prime ideals.
