(a) On an affine formal open subset $U$, the sheaf $\mathcal F'/\mathcal J^n\mathcal F'$ is coherent on the affine scheme $(U,\mathcal O_U/\mathcal J^n)$. Given a section of $\mathcal F''$ on $U$, choose a finite distinguished affine cover of the reduction and local lifts to $\mathcal F/\mathcal J^n\mathcal F'$. Their differences form a cocycle in this coherent sheaf. The localization argument in the proof of (5.6) makes this cocycle a coboundary, so the corrected lifts glue. Thus
\[0\to\Gamma(U,\mathcal F'/\mathcal J^n\mathcal F')\to\Gamma(U,\mathcal F/\mathcal J^n\mathcal F')\to\Gamma(U,\mathcal F'')\to0\]
 is exact.

(b) The transition maps on $\Gamma(U,\mathcal F'/\mathcal J^n\mathcal F')$ are surjective by (5.6), so (9.1), (9.2), and (9.6) give
\[0\to\Gamma(U,\mathcal F')\to\Gamma(U,\varprojlim_n\mathcal F/\mathcal J^n\mathcal F')\to\Gamma(U,\mathcal F'')\to0.\]
These sequences on affine formal open subsets give an exact sequence of sheaves. The natural map from the original sequence to it is the identity on $\mathcal F'$ and $\mathcal F''$, hence $\mathcal F\cong\varprojlim_n\mathcal F/\mathcal J^n\mathcal F'$. Taking $U=\mathfrak X$ gives the required exactness.
