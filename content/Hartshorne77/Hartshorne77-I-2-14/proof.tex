Every point of the image has all $2\times2$ minors of $(z_{ij})$ equal to zero. Conversely, if all minors vanish and $b_{rs}\ne0$, then $b_{ij}=b_{is}b_{rj}/b_{rs}$. Thus $(b_{ij})$ is the image of $((b_{is})_i,(b_{rj})_j)$. The image is therefore the closed set defined by the minors. Its homogeneous ideal is the kernel of $k[z_{ij}]\to k[x_i,y_j]$, since a polynomial vanishing on all products $x_i y_j$ becomes the zero polynomial over the infinite field $k$. This kernel is prime, so the image is a projective variety.
