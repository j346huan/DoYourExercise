The parametrized set is $V(y^2-xz,yz-x^3,z^2-x^2y)$. Indeed, if $x=0$, these equations give $y=z=0$; otherwise $t=y/x$ satisfies $t^3=x,t^4=y,t^5=z$. Its ideal is the kernel of $k[x,y,z]\to k[t]$, sending $(x,y,z)$ to $(t^3,t^4,t^5)$. This ideal is prime and its quotient has transcendence degree $1$, so its height is $2$.

Let $\mathfrak m=(x,y,z)$. No nonzero element of $I(Y)$ has a constant or linear term, since the weights $0,3,4,5$ cannot occur among monomials of ordinary degree at least $2$. Thus $\mathfrak m I(Y)\subseteq\mathfrak m^3$. The quadratic terms $y^2-xz,yz,z^2$ of the three displayed polynomials are linearly independent. Their classes in $I(Y)/\mathfrak m I(Y)$ are therefore independent, so $I(Y)$ requires at least three generators.
