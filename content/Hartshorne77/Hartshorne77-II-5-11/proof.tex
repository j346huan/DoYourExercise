Write $B=S\times_AT$. Given homogeneous $f\in S_d$ and $g\in T_d$ of the same positive degree, localization gives
\[B_{(f\otimes g)}\cong S_{(f)}\otimes_AT_{(g)},\qquad \frac{s\otimes t}{(f\otimes g)^n}\mapsto\frac{s}{f^n}\otimes\frac{t}{g^n}.\]
The inverse sends $(s/f^m)\otimes(t/g^n)$ to $(sf^n\otimes tg^m)/(f\otimes g)^{m+n}$. These charts cover $\operatorname{Proj}B$: the positive-degree pure tensors generate $B_+$ as an ideal. They also cover $X\times_AY$, since powers make the degrees of any pair of positive-degree homogeneous elements equal. The isomorphisms commute with further localization, so they glue to $\operatorname{Proj}B\cong X\times_AY$.

The same calculation, with $\deg s=nd+1$ and $\deg t=nd+1$, gives
\[(B(1))_{(f\otimes g)}\cong(S(1))_{(f)}\otimes_A(T(1))_{(g)}.\]
Indeed, denominators in any elementary tensor on the right can be increased to a common exponent. These compatible identifications give $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Proj}B}(1)\cong p_1^*\mathcal O_X(1)\otimes p_2^*\mathcal O_Y(1)$.
