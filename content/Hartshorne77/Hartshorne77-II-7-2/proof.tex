Put $r=\dim_kV$ and choose a basis $v_1,\ldots,v_r$ of $V$. Invertible changes of coordinates turn the two tuples of sections into $(v_1,\ldots,v_r,0,\ldots,0)$, of lengths $n+1$ and $m+1$. Projection onto the first $n+1$ coordinates carries the second tuple to the first. Its center has dimension $m-n-1$ and misses the image, since the $v_i$ generate $\mathcal L$. Conjugating this projection by the coordinate change in $\mathbb P^m$, and then applying the inverse coordinate change in $\mathbb P^n$, gives the required factorization.
