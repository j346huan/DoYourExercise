Pass to its Boolean ring $A$ using \exref{1.24}. By \exref{1.23}, $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is compact Hausdorff and every clopen subset has the form $D(f)$. The map $f\mapsto D(f)$ preserves meets, joins and complements by \exref{1.17} and \exref{1.23}.

Moreover, $D(f)\subseteq D(g)$ iff $D(f(1-g))=\varnothing$, iff $f(1-g)$ is nilpotent. A Boolean ring has no nonzero nilpotents, so this is equivalent to $f=fg$, namely $f\leq g$. Thus the map is an order isomorphism onto the clopen subsets.
