The polynomial ring $A[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ is free, hence flat, over $A$. It is Noetherian, and its $(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$-adic completion is $A[[x_1,\ldots,x_n]]$, which is flat over it by Proposition 10.14. Thus the power series ring is flat over $A$.

Evaluation at $x_1=\cdots=x_n=0$ is a retraction onto $A$. For any $A$-module $M$, it splits the natural map $M\to M\otimes_A A[[x_1,\ldots,x_n]]$. Therefore tensoring with the power series ring cannot annihilate a nonzero module, proving faithful flatness.
