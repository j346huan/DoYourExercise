Let $\mathfrak{m}$ be the maximal ideal and $k=A/\mathfrak{m}$. By \exref{2.2} and associativity of tensor products,
\[(M/\mathfrak{m}M)\otimes_k(N/\mathfrak{m}N)\cong k\otimes_A(M\otimes_A N)=0.\]
The dimension of the left side is the product of the dimensions of its two factors. Hence one factor is zero, and Nakayama's lemma gives $M=0$ or $N=0$.
