For a proper ideal $I$, the algebra $A=k[t_1,\ldots,t_n]/I$ is nonzero. By \exref{5.16}, it is integral over a polynomial subalgebra $k[y_1,\ldots,y_r]$. Evaluation at $y_1=\cdots=y_r=0$ extends to a homomorphism $A\to k$ by \exref{5.2}. If $a_i$ is the image of $t_i$, then every polynomial in $I$ vanishes at $(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$.

If $I$ is maximal, it is contained in the maximal ideal $(t_1-a_1,\ldots,t_n-a_n)$, so they are equal. Conversely, each such ideal has quotient $k$, hence is maximal.
