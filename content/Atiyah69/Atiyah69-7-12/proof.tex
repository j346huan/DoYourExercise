For an increasing chain of ideals $\mathfrak{a}_1\subseteq\mathfrak{a}_2\subseteq\cdots$ in $A$, the extended chain $\mathfrak{a}_1B\subseteq\mathfrak{a}_2B\subseteq\cdots$ stabilizes. Faithful flatness gives $\mathfrak{a}_iB\cap A=\mathfrak{a}_i$ by \exref{3.16}, so contraction makes the original chain stabilize.
