Since $pA=0$, the $p$-adic topology on $A$ is discrete and $\widehat A=A$. In the given embedding, the $j$th summand lies in $p^nB$ exactly when $j>n$. Thus
\[A\cap p^nB=\bigoplus_{j>n}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z},\qquad A/(A\cap p^nB)\cong\bigoplus_{j=1}^n\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}.\]
The inverse limit of these quotients is $\prod_{j\ge1}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, which is the completion for the induced topology.

Put $C=B/A$. For every $n$ there is an exact sequence $0\to A/(A\cap p^nB)\to B/p^nB\to C/p^nC\to0$. The transition maps of the first inverse system are surjective, so Proposition 10.2 gives
\[0\to\prod_{j\ge1}\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\to\widehat B\to\widehat C\to0.\]
The image of $\widehat A=A$ in this kernel is only the direct sum. Hence $\widehat A\to\widehat B\to\widehat C\to0$ is not exact at $\widehat B$, so $p$-adic completion is not right exact.
