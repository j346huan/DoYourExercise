The condition is immediate when $E$ is open. Conversely, suppose it holds and choose a minimal closed $X_0$ for which $E\cap X_0$ is not open. If $X_0=Y\cup Z$ with $Y,Z$ proper closed subsets, then $Y\setminus E$ and $Z\setminus E$ are closed by minimality. Their union $X_0\setminus E$ is closed, a contradiction. Hence $X_0$ is irreducible.

The intersection $E\cap X_0$ is nonempty, so it contains a nonempty open subset $U$ of $X_0$. By minimality, $E\cap(X_0\setminus U)$ is open in the proper closed subset $X_0\setminus U$. Its complement there is $X_0\setminus E$, which is therefore closed in $X_0$. This is again a contradiction.
