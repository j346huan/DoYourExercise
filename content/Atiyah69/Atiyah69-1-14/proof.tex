Has maximal elements by Zorn's lemma. Suppose $\mathfrak p$ is maximal element, and $xy\in\mathfrak p$. Then $axy=0$ for some $a\in A$. Now if $x,y\notin\mathfrak p$, then $(x)+\mathfrak p$, $(y)+\mathfrak p$ contain non-zero-divisors $f,g$, and $fg$ is not a zero divisor, but
\[
fg\in(xy)+(x)\mathfrak p+(y)\mathfrak p+\mathfrak p^2\subseteq(xy)+\mathfrak p=\mathfrak p,
\]
a contradiction. Thus $x\in\mathfrak p$ or $y\in\mathfrak p$.
