Since nilradical is contained in Jacobson radical, we show the other inclusion. Suppose $f$ is in the Jacobson radical, so $1+fg$ is a unit for all $g$ by prop 1.9. Take $g=x$ and get $1+f$ is a unit, so by \exref{1.2}(i) $a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_n$ are all nilpotent, and by \exref{1.2}(ii) $f$ is nilpotent.
