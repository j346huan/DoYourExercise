Since $\mathbb{Z}[t]/\mathfrak{m}\cong\mathbb{F}_2$, $\mathfrak{m}$ is maximal. We have $\mathfrak{q}\subseteq\mathfrak{m}$, $2^2\in\mathfrak{q}$ and $t\in\mathfrak{q}$, so $r(\mathfrak{q})=\mathfrak{m}$. Thus $\mathfrak{q}$ is $\mathfrak{m}$-primary by Proposition 4.2. Also $2\notin\mathfrak{q}$, so $\mathfrak{q}\ne\mathfrak{m}$, whereas $t\notin\mathfrak{m}^2=(4,2t,t^2)$. Hence $\mathfrak{q}\ne\mathfrak{m}^n$ for every $n\ge2$.
