\textbf{(i)} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{(ii)} follows from Proposition 3.16: a prime $\mathfrak p$ is contracted from a prime of $B$ exactly when $\mathfrak p^{ec}=\mathfrak p$.

\textbf{(ii)} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{(iii)}. Choose a prime $\mathfrak q\subseteq B$ contracting to $\mathfrak m$. Then $\mathfrak mB\subseteq\mathfrak q\ne B$.

\textbf{(iii)} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{(iv)}. For $0\ne x\in M$, write $Ax\cong A/\mathfrak a$ with $\mathfrak a$ proper. Choose a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m\supseteq\mathfrak a$. Then $B\otimes_A Ax\cong B/\mathfrak aB\ne0$, since $\mathfrak aB\subseteq\mathfrak mB\ne B$. Flatness makes this module a submodule of $B\otimes_A M$.

\textbf{(iv)} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{(v)}. Let $N$ be the kernel of $M\to B\otimes_A M$. Flatness gives an exact sequence $0\to B\otimes_A N\to B\otimes_A M\to B\otimes_A B\otimes_A M$, where the last map sends $b\otimes x$ to $b\otimes1\otimes x$. Multiplication of the two $B$ factors is a left inverse, so $B\otimes_A N=0$. By (iv), $N=0$.

\textbf{(v)} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{(i)}. Apply (v) to $A/\mathfrak a$. The kernel of $A/\mathfrak a\to B/\mathfrak aB$ is $\mathfrak a^{ec}/\mathfrak a$, so it is zero.
