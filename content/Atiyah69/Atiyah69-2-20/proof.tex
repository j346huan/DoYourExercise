Put $M=\varinjlim_iM_i$ and $P=\varinjlim_i(M_i\otimes_A N)$, with structure maps $\mu_i$ and $\nu_i$. By \exref{2.16}, the compatible maps $\mu_i\otimes1$ induce $\psi:P\to M\otimes_A N$.

Define $g:M\times N\to P$ by $g(\mu_i(x),y)=\nu_i(x\otimes y)$. If two representatives have the same image in $M$, then by \exref{2.15} they agree at some common stage, so this formula is well-defined. Passing to a common stage also shows additivity in the first variable; additivity in the second variable and $A$-bilinearity follow from the tensor product relations. Thus $g$ induces $\varphi:M\otimes_A N\to P$.

The formulas give $\psi\varphi(\mu_i(x)\otimes y)=\mu_i(x)\otimes y$ and $\varphi\psi(\nu_i(x\otimes y))=\nu_i(x\otimes y)$. These elements generate the respective modules, so $\psi$ and $\varphi$ are inverses.
