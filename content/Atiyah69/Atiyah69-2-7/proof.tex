By \exref{2.5}, tensoring $0\to\mathfrak{p}\to A\to A/\mathfrak{p}\to0$ with $A[x]$ remains exact. By \exref{2.6}, this gives $A[x]/\mathfrak{p}[x]\cong(A/\mathfrak{p})[x]$, which is an integral domain. Thus $\mathfrak{p}[x]$ is prime.

If $\mathfrak{m}$ is maximal, $A[x]/\mathfrak{m}[x]\cong(A/\mathfrak{m})[x]$ is not a field, since $x$ is nonzero and not invertible. Hence $\mathfrak{m}[x]$ is not maximal.
