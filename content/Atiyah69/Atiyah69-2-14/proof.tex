Let $C=\bigoplus_iM_i$ and let $D$ be generated by the elements $x-\mu_{ij}(x)$, with the two terms placed in the $i$-th and $j$-th summands. Since $D$ is a submodule, $C/D$ is an $A$-module. The maps $\mu_i:M_i\to C/D$, $x\mapsto x+D$, are $A$-linear, and for $i\leq j$ the defining relations give $\mu_i(x)-\mu_j(\mu_{ij}(x))=0$. Thus $\mu_i=\mu_j\mu_{ij}$, as required.
