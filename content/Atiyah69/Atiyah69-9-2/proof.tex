The equality is immediate if either polynomial is zero. Otherwise localize at a maximal ideal $\mathfrak p$ and choose a uniformizer $t$ of $A_{\mathfrak p}$. Write $f=t^rF$ and $g=t^sG$, where $r,s$ are the least valuations of their coefficients. Each of $F,G\in A_{\mathfrak p}[x]$ has a unit coefficient. Their reductions modulo $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ are nonzero polynomials over a field, so their product is nonzero. Thus $FG$ also has a unit coefficient, and
\[c(fg)A_{\mathfrak p}=(t^{r+s})=c(f)A_{\mathfrak p}\,c(g)A_{\mathfrak p}.\]
The ideals are therefore equal after localization at every maximal ideal, and hence equal in $A$.
