By \exref{5.16}, $B$ is integral over a polynomial subalgebra $k[y_1,\ldots,y_r]$ with the $y_i$ algebraically independent. Since $B$ is a field, Proposition 5.7 makes this subalgebra a field, so $r=0$. Thus $B$ is algebraic over $k$. Its finitely many algebra generators are algebraic, so adjoining them successively gives $[B:k]<\infty$.
