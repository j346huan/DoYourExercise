Suppose $f^*$ is closed. The image of $V(\mathfrak q)$ is a closed set containing $f^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$, so it contains $V(f^{-1}(\mathfrak q))$. Every prime $\mathfrak p\supseteq f^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$ therefore lifts to some $\mathfrak q'\supseteq\mathfrak q$.

Conversely, assume going-up. Every closed subset of $\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ is a finite union $\bigcup_iV(\mathfrak q_i)$ by \exref{6.7}. Contraction gives $f^*(V(\mathfrak q_i))\subseteq V(f^{-1}(\mathfrak q_i))$, and going-up gives the reverse inclusion. Its image is therefore the finite union $\bigcup_iV(f^{-1}(\mathfrak q_i))$, which is closed.
