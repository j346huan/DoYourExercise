For $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(A)$, \exref{3.21} identifies the fiber of $h^*$ with the spectrum of
\[(B\otimes_A C)\otimes_A k(\mathfrak p)\cong(B\otimes_A k(\mathfrak p))\otimes_{k(\mathfrak p)}(C\otimes_A k(\mathfrak p)).\]
A ring has nonempty spectrum exactly when it is nonzero, and a tensor product of vector spaces over a field is nonzero exactly when both factors are nonzero. Thus this fiber is nonempty exactly when both $B\otimes_A k(\mathfrak p)$ and $C\otimes_A k(\mathfrak p)$ are nonzero, or equivalently $\mathfrak p$ belongs to both images.
