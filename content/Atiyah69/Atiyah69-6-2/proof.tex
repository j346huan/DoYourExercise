For any submodule $M'\subseteq M$, the collection of its finitely generated submodules is nonempty and therefore has a maximal member $N$. If $x\in M'\setminus N$, then $N+Ax$ is a larger finitely generated submodule, a contradiction. Thus $M'=N$ is finitely generated. Hence every submodule of $M$ is finitely generated, so $M$ is Noetherian.
