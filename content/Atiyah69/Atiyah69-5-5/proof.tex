(i) If $x\in A$ has inverse in $B$, write $x^{-n}+a_1x^{-(n-1)}+\cdots+a_n=0$ with $a_i\in A$. Multiplying by $x^{n-1}$ expresses $x^{-1}$ as an element of $A$.

(ii) Every maximal ideal of $B$ contracts to a maximal ideal of $A$, and every maximal ideal of $A$ is the contraction of a maximal ideal of $B$, by lying-over and Corollary 5.8. Taking intersections gives $J(B)\cap A=J(A)$.
