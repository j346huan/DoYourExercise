If $b'\in B'$ satisfies a monic equation with coefficients in $f(B)$, then $b'\otimes1$ satisfies the same equation with each coefficient tensored with $1$. Thus $b'\otimes1$ is integral over the image of $B\otimes_A C$. Since $1\otimes c$ belongs to that image, every pure tensor $b'\otimes c$ is integral over it. Integral elements form a subring, so every finite sum of pure tensors is integral.
