By \exref{3.28}, the constructible topology is generated by the sets $D(a)$ and $V(a)$. Their inverse images under $f^*$ are $D(f(a))$ and $V(f(a))$, respectively, so $f^*$ is continuous. Both spectra are compact Hausdorff spaces by \exref{3.28}. A closed subset of the source is compact, hence its image is compact and therefore closed in the target.
