Let $\mathfrak{a}\ne0$ and choose $0\ne x\in\mathfrak{a}$. Only finitely many maximal ideals contain $x$. For each of them, choose finitely many elements of $\mathfrak{a}$ whose images generate $\mathfrak{a}_{\mathfrak{m}}$, which is possible because $A_{\mathfrak{m}}$ is Noetherian. Let $\mathfrak{a}_0$ be generated by all these elements and $x$.

At the chosen maximal ideals, $\mathfrak{a}_0$ and $\mathfrak{a}$ have the same localization. At every other maximal ideal, $x$ is a unit, so both localizations are the whole local ring. Hence $(\mathfrak{a}/\mathfrak{a}_0)_{\mathfrak{m}}=0$ for every maximal ideal, giving $\mathfrak{a}=\mathfrak{a}_0$. Thus every ideal is finitely generated.
