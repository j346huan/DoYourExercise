An element of $M$ is represented by a finite sum $\sum_{r=1}^n x_r$ with $x_r\in M_{i_r}$. Choose $j\geq i_r$ for every $r$. Its image is $\mu_j(\sum_r\mu_{i_rj}(x_r))$.

If $\mu_i(x)=0$, then $x$, viewed in the direct sum, is a finite sum of relations $y-\mu_{jk}(y)$. Choose $l\geq i$ and all indices occurring in these relations. Map every involved summand $M_r$ to $M_l$ by $\mu_{rl}$. Each relation maps to zero, so $\mu_{il}(x)=0$. The converse follows from $\mu_i=\mu_j\mu_{ij}$.
