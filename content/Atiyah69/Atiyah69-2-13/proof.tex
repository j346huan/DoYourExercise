The balanced map $(b,y)\mapsto by$ induces $p:B\otimes_A N\to N$. Since $pg=1$, the $A$-linear map $g$ is injective. Every $z\in B\otimes_A N$ has the decomposition $z=g(p(z))+(z-g(p(z)))$, with the second term in $\ker p$. If $g(y)\in\ker p$, then $y=pg(y)=0$. Thus $B\otimes_A N=g(N)\oplus\ker p$ as $A$-modules.
