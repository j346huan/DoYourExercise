Write $\mathfrak{a}=\bigcap_i\mathfrak{q}_i$ minimally, with $r(\mathfrak{q}_i)=\mathfrak{p}_i$. Localization at $S_f$ discards exactly those components for which $f\in\mathfrak{p}_i$. Contracting gives $S_f(\mathfrak{a})=\mathfrak{q}_{\Sigma}$.

Also $(\mathfrak{a}:f^n)=\bigcap_i(\mathfrak{q}_i:f^n)$. If $f\notin\mathfrak{p}_i$, then $(\mathfrak{q}_i:f^n)=\mathfrak{q}_i$ for every $n$. If $f\in\mathfrak{p}_i$, some power of $f$ lies in $\mathfrak{q}_i$, so $(\mathfrak{q}_i:f^n)=A$ for all sufficiently large $n$. Choose $n$ large enough for every component.
