By \exref{5.16}, choose a polynomial subring $B=k[y_1,\ldots,y_d]\subseteq A$ over which $A$ is integral. Its fraction field has algebraic extension $\operatorname{Frac}A$, so $d=\operatorname{trdeg}_k\operatorname{Frac}A$.

For a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $A$, its contraction $\mathfrak n$ is maximal in $B$. Since $B$ is integrally closed, Lemma 11.26 gives $\dim A_{\mathfrak m}=\dim B_{\mathfrak n}$.

Let $\overline k$ be an algebraic closure of $k$. The domain $\overline k[y_1,\ldots,y_d]$ is integral over $B$, since its coefficients are algebraic over $k$. Choose a maximal ideal $\mathfrak N$ above $\mathfrak n$. Applying Lemma 11.26 again gives
\[\dim B_{\mathfrak n}=\dim\overline k[y_1,\ldots,y_d]_{\mathfrak N}=d,\]
where the last equality follows from Theorem 11.25 over $\overline k$.
