The nilradical of $A$ is nilpotent by Proposition 7.15. If all $a_n$ lie in it, some fixed power annihilates every product of that many coefficients, so the same power of $f$ is zero.

Conversely, suppose $f$ is nilpotent. Modulo any prime $\mathfrak{p}$, its image lies in the domain $(A/\mathfrak{p})[[x]]$ and is therefore zero. Hence every $a_n$ belongs to every prime ideal, so is nilpotent.
