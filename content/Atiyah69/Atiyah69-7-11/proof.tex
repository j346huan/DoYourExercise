No. Take $A=\prod_{n\ge1}\mathbb{F}_2$. If $e_n$ is $1$ in coordinate $n$ and $0$ elsewhere, the ideals $(e_1)\subsetneq(e_1,e_2)\subsetneq\cdots$ never stabilize.

The ring $A$ is Boolean, and so is each $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$. A local ring has no idempotents other than $0,1$ by \exref{1.12}. Thus $A_{\mathfrak{p}}\cong\mathbb{F}_2$, which is Noetherian.
