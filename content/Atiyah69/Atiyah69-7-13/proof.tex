The fiber over $\mathfrak{p}$ is homeomorphic to $\operatorname{Spec}(k(\mathfrak{p})\otimes_A B)$ with its subspace topology. The images of finitely many $A$-algebra generators of $B$ generate this fiber ring as a $k(\mathfrak{p})$-algebra. Hilbert's basis theorem makes it Noetherian, so its spectrum is Noetherian by \exref{6.8}.
