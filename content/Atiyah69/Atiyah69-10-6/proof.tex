The closure of a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ is $\bigcap_{n\ge1}(\mathfrak{m}+\mathfrak{a}^n)$. If $\mathfrak{a}\subseteq\mathfrak{m}$, every term equals $\mathfrak{m}$, so it is closed. If $\mathfrak{a}\not\subseteq\mathfrak{m}$, the image of $\mathfrak{a}$ in the field $A/\mathfrak{m}$ is the whole field, and the same holds for every power. Hence the closure is $A$, so $\mathfrak{m}$ is not closed. Therefore every maximal ideal is closed exactly when $\mathfrak{a}\subseteq J(A)$.
