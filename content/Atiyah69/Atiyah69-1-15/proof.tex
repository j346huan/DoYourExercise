A prime contains $E$ iff it contains $(E)$, and it contains an ideal iff it contains its radical. Every prime contains $0$ and no prime contains $1$. Containment of $\bigcup_iE_i$ is equivalent to containment of every $E_i$.

If a prime contains $\mathfrak a\mathfrak b$ but not $\mathfrak a$, choose $a\in\mathfrak a\setminus\mathfrak p$. Then $a\mathfrak b\subseteq\mathfrak p$ forces $\mathfrak b\subseteq\mathfrak p$. Conversely, containing either factor implies containing their product and intersection. Since $\mathfrak a\mathfrak b\subseteq\mathfrak a\cap\mathfrak b$, the required equalities follow. These identities give the closed-set axioms for the Zariski topology.
