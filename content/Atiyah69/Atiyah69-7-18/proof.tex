Since $A$ is Noetherian, some power of $\mathfrak{p}$ annihilates $M/Q_i$, hence annihilates $H$. Choose the least $n>0$ with $\mathfrak{p}^nH=0$ and take $0\ne x\in\mathfrak{p}^{n-1}H$. Then $\mathfrak{p}x=0$. Every element outside $\mathfrak{p}$ acts injectively on $M/Q_i$, and therefore on $H$, so $\operatorname{Ann}(x)=\mathfrak{p}$.

\textbf{(ii) $\Leftrightarrow$ (iii)} The cyclic submodule $Ax$ is isomorphic to $A/\operatorname{Ann}(x)$; conversely, take the image of $1$ under an embedding $A/\mathfrak{p}\to M$.
