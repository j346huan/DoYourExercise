(i) If $f:L'\to L$ is injective, tensoring first with $M$ and then with $N$ preserves injectivity. By associativity, the resulting map is $f\otimes1_{M\otimes_A N}$. Thus $M\otimes_A N$ is flat.

(ii) For every $A$-module $L$, associativity gives $L\otimes_A N\cong(L\otimes_A B)\otimes_B N$. Tensoring an injection with $B$ preserves injectivity over $A$, and then tensoring with $N$ preserves injectivity over $B$. Hence $N$ is flat over $A$.
