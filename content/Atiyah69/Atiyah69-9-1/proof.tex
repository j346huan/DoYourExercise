The ring $B=S^{-1}A$ is Noetherian and integrally closed. By the prime correspondence, every nonzero prime of $B$ is maximal. Thus $B$ is a Dedekind domain unless it is a field, in which case $B=\operatorname{Frac}(A)$.

Extension preserves products and sends a principal fractional ideal $(x)$ to $xB$. It therefore induces a homomorphism of ideal class groups. Every nonzero fractional ideal of $B$ is finitely generated, say $J=\sum_iBx_i$ with $x_i\in\operatorname{Frac}(A)$. The module $\sum_iAx_i$ is a fractional ideal of $A$, and its extension is $J$. Hence the homomorphism is surjective, also when the target class group is trivial.
