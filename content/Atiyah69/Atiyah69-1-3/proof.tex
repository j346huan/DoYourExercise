Let $f\in R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$. Write $f=g_0+g_1x_n+\cdots+g_mx_n^m$ where $g_i\in R[x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1}]$.

By \exref{1.2}(i) $f$ is a unit iff $g_0$ is a unit and $g_i$ are nilpotent for $i\geq 1$. Observe $g_0$ now has one less variable, and again, an induction on $n$ shows that $f$ is a unit iff the constant term is a unit and other coefficients over $R$ are nilpotent.

(ii), (iii) can also be done by induction on number of indeterminates.

(iv) is the same proof, when we take minimal element, we take it with respect to lexicographic order.
