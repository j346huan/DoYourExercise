Let $M\to M'$ be an injection of $A$-modules and let $N$ be the kernel of $B\otimes_A M\to B\otimes_A M'$. Since $C$ is flat over $B$, tensoring gives an exact sequence $0\to C\otimes_B N\to C\otimes_A M\to C\otimes_A M'$. The last map is injective because $C$ is flat over $A$. Thus $C\otimes_B N=0$, and faithful flatness gives $N=0$ by \exref{3.16}. Therefore tensoring with $B$ preserves injections.
