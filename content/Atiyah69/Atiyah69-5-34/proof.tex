Put $C=g(B)$, so $A\subseteq C\subseteq K$. Since $C$ is a quotient of $B$, the map $\operatorname{Spec}C\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is closed. Choose a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{n}$ of $C$. Its closed singleton maps to the closed singleton $\{\mathfrak{n}\cap A\}$, so $\mathfrak{n}\cap A$ is the maximal ideal of $A$. Thus $C_{\mathfrak{n}}$ dominates $A$. By \exref{5.27}, $C_{\mathfrak{n}}=A$. Since $A\subseteq C\subseteq C_{\mathfrak{n}}$, we get $C=A$.
