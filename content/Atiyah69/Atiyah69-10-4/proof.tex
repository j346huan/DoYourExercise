Complete the exact sequence $0\to A\xrightarrow{x}A\to A/xA\to0$. Exactness of completion for finitely generated modules gives an injection $\widehat A\xrightarrow{\widehat x}\widehat A$.

A completion of a domain need not be a domain. Take $A=\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathfrak{a}=(6)$. The Chinese remainder theorem gives $\mathbb{Z}/6^n\mathbb{Z}\cong\mathbb{Z}/2^n\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}/3^n\mathbb{Z}$ compatibly with reduction, hence $\widehat A\cong\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_3$. The nonzero elements $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ have product zero.
