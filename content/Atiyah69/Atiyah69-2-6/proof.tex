Define multiplication by distributing and setting $(ax^i)(mx^j)=(am)x^{i+j}$. The module axioms follow coefficientwise from those of $M$ and the associativity of finite sums.

The $A$-bilinear map $(f,m)\mapsto fm$ induces an $A[x]$-linear map $A[x]\otimes_A M\to M[x]$. Its inverse is $\sum_i m_ix^i\mapsto\sum_i x^i\otimes m_i$. Both compositions are the identity, since $ax^i\otimes m=x^i\otimes am$.
