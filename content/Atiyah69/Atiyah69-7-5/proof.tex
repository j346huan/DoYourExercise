We have $A\to B^G\subseteq B$, and $B$ is integral over $B^G$ by \exref{5.12}. The finitely many $A$-algebra generators of $B$ also generate it over $B^G$, so integrality makes $B$ a finite $B^G$-module. The Artin–Tate lemma, Proposition 7.8, now shows that $B^G$ is a finitely generated $A$-algebra.
