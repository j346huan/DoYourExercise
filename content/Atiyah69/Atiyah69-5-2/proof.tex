Put $\mathfrak{p}=\ker f$. By lying-over, choose $\mathfrak{q}\subseteq B$ with $\mathfrak{q}\cap A=\mathfrak{p}$. Then $B/\mathfrak{q}$ is integral over $A/\mathfrak{p}$, so its fraction field is algebraic over the fraction field of $A/\mathfrak{p}$. The embedding $A/\mathfrak{p}\to\Omega$ extends to its fraction field and then to this algebraic extension, since $\Omega$ is algebraically closed. Composing with $B\to B/\mathfrak{q}$ gives the required map.
