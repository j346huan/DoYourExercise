A free module is flat, and flatness gives (iii). Tensoring $0\to\mathfrak{m}\to A\to k\to0$ with $M$ gives
\[0\to\operatorname{Tor}_1^A(k,M)\to\mathfrak{m}\otimes_A M\to A\otimes_A M,\]
so (iii) and (iv) are equivalent.

Assume (iv). Lift a basis of $M/\mathfrak{m}M$ to $x_1,\ldots,x_n\in M$. Nakayama's lemma makes these generators. Let $F=A^n\to M$ send the standard basis to the $x_i$, and let $E$ be its kernel. The module $E$ is finitely generated because $A$ is Noetherian. The Tor sequence gives $0\to k\otimes_A E\to k\otimes_A F\to k\otimes_A M\to0$. The last map is an isomorphism by the choice of the $x_i$, so $E/\mathfrak{m}E=0$. Nakayama gives $E=0$, hence $M\cong A^n$.
